# Prognosis category formatting

- Maximum 50 words across the completed `text` fields.
- Use full sentences.
- Answer only: **How does each reportable NGS result affect prognosis in this patient?**
- Integrate variants that share the same prognostic effect when citation provenance remains correct.
- Name the applicable disease-specific prognostic framework when the retained rules support it.
- Do not add score-calculation commentary or routine negatives unless a retained prognosis rule makes them independently reportable.
