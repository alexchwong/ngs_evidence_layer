# Treatment category formatting

- Maximum 50 words across the completed `text` fields.
- Use full sentences.
- Answer only: **How may treatment be affected by the reportable NGS result(s), if at all?**
- Prioritise actionable, disease- and setting-specific implications supported by retained R3 rules.
- Do not restate prognosis, diagnosis, MRD or germline conclusions except for the minimum context required to make the treatment implication intelligible.
