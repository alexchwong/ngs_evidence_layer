# Expected — Case 1

Exercises: `escalates_to` moving the disease off the provisional label; both
classifiers' NPM1 cards coexisting rather than being reconciled; the FLT3 card
resisting treatment as entity-defining.

Question for the panel: does the NPM1 result change the diagnosis, and what
follows from it?

Expected behaviour:
- The integrated diagnosis should change from provisional MDS when the retrieved NPM1 diagnostic criteria are satisfied.
- NPM1 classification evidence should drive that diagnostic change; FLT3 should not independently redefine the disease merely because it is present.
- The final report should use the revised integrated diagnosis and the downstream evidence retrieved for that diagnosis.
