## Evidence semantic bridge

Support does not require verbatim restatement of the clinical conclusion. A card may support a proposition when it states an authoritative rule, classification criterion, or defining criterion and the supplied fact provides the corresponding finding, such that the clinical conclusion follows directly from applying that rule.

Allowed semantic bridges include:
- ordinary paraphrase with unchanged clinical meaning;
- synonymous or canonical disease/subtype naming;
- an abnormality-defined classification label versus the corresponding canonical disease entity;
- a source rule or defining criterion versus its direct application to the supplied patient finding.

These bridges are allowed only when they preserve all clinically material restrictions.

Do not infer, add, or relax any missing material restriction. Material restrictions include, where relevant:
- allelic state, including monoallelic versus biallelic/multihit;
- number of variants or hits;
- loss of heterozygosity or copy-number state;
- VAF or other thresholds;
- variant class;
- disease or subtype context;
- treatment or exposure context;
- required co-mutations or exclusions;
- cytogenetic context;
- population or endpoint;
- framework/source attribution;
- polarity, uncertainty, and evidentiary strength.

A difference in nomenclature may be bridged. A difference in a clinically material restriction may not.

Before returning no match, re-check every supplied card for the same framework/source, disease/entity, defining molecular abnormality, and clinical function. A card explicitly naming the same disease, defining abnormality, and clinical function should normally be selected unless a material restriction conflicts.

Examples:
- PML::RARA: a classification rule for the PML::RARA-defined AML/APL entity may support a proposition applying that rule to diagnose the corresponding PML::RARA-defined APL entity; this is a nomenclature/direct-rule bridge.
- TP53: evidence specific to biallelic/multihit TP53 does not support an unspecified or monoallelic TP53 state. Allelic state is a material restriction, not nomenclature.
