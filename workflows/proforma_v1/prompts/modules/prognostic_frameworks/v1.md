## Accepted prognostic framework preset

Only the following named systems are treated as prognostic frameworks in this workflow. Use the exact framework name shown and only in the applicable disease/context. A paper title, card nickname, author-year label, review, cohort study, diagnostic classification, or recommendation document is not a prognostic framework unless its named framework appears in this preset.

- AML: `ELN 2022 genetic risk classification`
- AML treated with less-intensive therapy: `ELN 2024 less-intensive genetic risk classification`
- MDS: `IPSS-M`
- CMML: `CPSS-Mol`
- Primary myelofibrosis: `MIPSS70`
- Primary myelofibrosis: `MIPSS70-plus`
- Primary myelofibrosis: `MIPSS70+ v2.0`
- Post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis: `MYSEC-PM`
- Essential thrombocythaemia: `MIPSS-ET`
- Polycythaemia vera: `MIPSS-PV`
- Essential thrombocythaemia thrombosis risk: `revised IPSET-thrombosis`
- CHIP/CCUS: `CHRS`

Framework applicability and framework tier assignment are patient-level assessments. They may use supplied morphology, cytogenetics, non-NGS molecular abnormalities, NGS findings, and clinical data as permitted by the framework. An empty NGS variant list does not by itself prevent framework assignment.
