- For `diagnosis_status: progress`, determine the underlying disease classification before interpreting current response. Treatment response, remission, reduced blasts, count recovery, or loss/non-detection of a previously diagnostic abnormality must not downgrade the established disease or reject historical molecular/cytogenetic subclassification.
- `progress` does not freeze the established disease label at its original specificity. Historical disease-defining molecular/cytogenetic findings that remain valid for classification may refine the established disease even when no longer detected after therapy.
- `progress` also does not block genuine progression or transformation. After the underlying diagnosis is fixed, interpret response, persistence, progression, or transformation; supported current findings may supersede, up-stage, or transform the established disease.
