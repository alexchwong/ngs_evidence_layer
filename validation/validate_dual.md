# NEL dual-pathology validation set

## Case 1 — MDS with concurrent hairy cell leukaemia

### Case summary

72M with Hb 88 g/L, WCC 2.4 ×10^9/L and platelets 74 ×10^9/L.

Bone marrow is hypercellular with multilineage dysplasia and 3% blasts, morphologically consistent with **myelodysplastic neoplasm (MDS)**.

**Cytogenetics:** Normal.

**NGS:** `ASXL1` frameshift, VAF 32%; `SRSF2` p.(Pro95His), VAF 29%; `BRAF` p.(Val600Glu), VAF 5%.

Flow cytometry identifies a small clonal B-cell population expressing CD19, bright CD20, CD103 and CD25.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve MDS as a supported myeloid diagnosis.
- **R1C2.** Recognise BRAF p.(Val600Glu) as a strong clue to hairy cell leukaemia in this setting.
- **R1C3.** Use the characteristic clonal B-cell population as independent support for concurrent hairy cell leukaemia.
- **R1C4.** Report MDS and hairy cell leukaemia as concurrent pathologies.
- **R1C5.** Do not infer that BRAF belongs to the MDS clone from bulk VAF.

## Case 2 — MDS with concurrent lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma

### Case summary

76M with macrocytic anaemia and thrombocytopenia.

Bone marrow shows multilineage dysplasia with 2% blasts, morphologically consistent with **MDS**.

**NGS:** `TET2` mutation, VAF 38%; `ASXL1` mutation, VAF 31%; `MYD88` p.(Leu265Pro), VAF 9%.

A small kappa-restricted B-cell population with plasmacytic differentiation is present. Serum studies identify an IgM paraprotein.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve MDS as the supported myeloid diagnosis.
- **R1C2.** Recognise MYD88 p.(Leu265Pro) as a strong clue to a lymphoplasmacytic neoplasm in this setting.
- **R1C3.** Use the kappa-restricted lymphoplasmacytic population as independent support for the concurrent lymphoid process.
- **R1C4.** Use the IgM paraprotein as independent support for the concurrent lymphoplasmacytic process.
- **R1C5.** Do not assign MYD88 p.(Leu265Pro) to the MDS clone from bulk sequencing alone.

## Case 3 — MDS with concurrent T-LGL leukaemia

### Case summary

69F with macrocytic anaemia and persistent severe neutropenia.

Bone marrow shows multilineage dysplasia with 2% blasts, morphologically consistent with **MDS**.

**NGS:** `STAG2` mutation, VAF 28%; `ASXL1` mutation, VAF 25%; `STAT3` p.(Tyr640Phe), VAF 11%.

Persistent circulating large granular lymphocytes are present. Flow cytometry identifies an abnormal CD3-positive, CD8-positive, CD57-positive T-cell population, with evidence of T-cell clonality.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve MDS as the supported myeloid diagnosis.
- **R1C2.** Recognise activating STAT3 p.(Tyr640Phe) as a strong clue to T-LGL leukaemia in this setting.
- **R1C3.** Use the persistent large granular lymphocytosis as independent support for the concurrent T-LGL process.
- **R1C4.** Use the abnormal CD3-positive/CD8-positive/CD57-positive T-cell population as independent support for the concurrent T-LGL process.
- **R1C5.** Use the supplied T-cell clonality as independent support for the concurrent T-LGL process.
- **R1C6.** Do not infer that STAT3 and the myeloid variants reside in the same clone from bulk VAF.

## Case 4 — CMML with concurrent systemic mastocytosis

### Case summary

74M with persistent peripheral blood monocytosis.

Bone marrow shows myelomonocytic proliferation, dysplasia and 5% blasts, morphologically consistent with **chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia (CMML)**.

**NGS:** `TET2` mutation, VAF 43%; `SRSF2` p.(Pro95His), VAF 38%; `ASXL1` mutation, VAF 26%; `KIT` p.(Asp816Val), VAF 8%.

Serum tryptase is 52 ng/mL. Review of the marrow identifies small aggregates of spindle-shaped mast cells with aberrant CD25 expression.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve CMML as the supported myeloid diagnosis.
- **R1C2.** Recognise KIT p.(Asp816Val) as a strong molecular clue to systemic mastocytosis.
- **R1C3.** Use the spindle-shaped mast-cell aggregates as independent support for systemic mastocytosis.
- **R1C4.** Use aberrant mast-cell CD25 expression as independent support for systemic mastocytosis.
- **R1C5.** Use the elevated serum tryptase as independent support for systemic mastocytosis.
- **R1C6.** Report systemic mastocytosis with an associated haematological neoplasm (CMML) where supported.

## Case 5 — MDS with concurrent VEXAS syndrome

### Case summary

70M with macrocytic anaemia, thrombocytopenia, recurrent fevers and auricular chondritis.

Bone marrow shows dysplasia with prominent cytoplasmic vacuolation of myeloid and erythroid precursors and is morphologically consistent with **low-blast MDS**.

**NGS:** `UBA1` p.(Met41Thr), VAF 36%; `DNMT3A` mutation, VAF 7%.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve MDS as the supported haematological diagnosis.
- **R1C2.** Recognise UBA1 p.(Met41Thr) as a molecular clue to VEXAS syndrome in this setting.
- **R1C3.** Use the late-onset inflammatory phenotype as independent support for VEXAS syndrome.
- **R1C4.** Use the characteristic marrow vacuolation as independent support for VEXAS syndrome.
- **R1C5.** Report VEXAS as a concurrent systemic inflammatory syndrome rather than a second haematological neoplasm.
- **R1C6.** Do not infer constitutional UBA1 status from tumour-only VAF.

## Case 6 — Myeloid neoplasm with concurrent B-lymphoblastic neoplasm

### Case summary

67M with pancytopenia.

Bone marrow is initially interpreted as a **high-grade myeloid neoplasm with dysplasia and an increased blast population**.

**NGS:** `ASXL1` mutation; `TET2` mutation; `IKZF1` p.(Asn159Tyr).

The unexpected IKZF1 p.(Asn159Tyr) finding prompts lineage-directed review. Flow cytometry identifies a distinct CD19-positive, CD10-positive, TdT-positive precursor B-cell population separate from the myeloid population.

### Marking criteria

#### R1 — Diagnosis and classification

- **R1C1.** Preserve the supplied myeloid neoplasm as a supported process unless the total clinicopathological evidence requires reclassification.
- **R1C2.** Recognise IKZF1 p.(Asn159Tyr) as a strong clue to a B-lymphoblastic neoplasm in this setting.
- **R1C3.** Use the distinct CD19-positive/CD10-positive/TdT-positive precursor population as independent support for a concurrent B-lymphoblastic process.
- **R1C4.** Do not diagnose mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia from the IKZF1 variant alone.
- **R1C5.** Do not infer shared clonality between the B-lymphoblasts and myeloid cells from bulk NGS alone.
