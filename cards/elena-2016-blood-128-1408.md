# Elena C, Gallì A, Such E, Meggendorfer M, Germing U, Rizzo E, et al. Integrating clinical features and genetic lesions in the risk assessment of patients with chronic myelomonocytic leukemia. Blood. 2016;128(10):1408-1417.

**Publication key:** `elena-2016-blood-128-1408`

## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, NRAS, RUNX1, SETBP1

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results—Prognostic value of genetic lesions in CMML

### Interpretation

In CMML, RUNX1, NRAS, SETBP1, and ASXL1 mutations each retained independent adverse prognostic value for overall survival after accounting for CPSS cytogenetic risk; these variables formed a CMML-specific genetic score that separated four groups for overall survival and AML evolution.

## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, NRAS, RUNX1, SETBP1

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results—Prognostic value of genetic lesions in CMML; Development and validation of CPSS-Mol

### Interpretation

CPSS-Mol combines the ASXL1/NRAS/RUNX1/SETBP1-plus-cytogenetic genetic score with RBC transfusion dependency, WBC count, and marrow blast percentage; sex-specific haemoglobin thresholds can substitute for transfusion dependency. The resulting four-group model stratified overall survival and leukemic evolution and was confirmed in an independent CMML validation cohort.

## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** EZH2

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Prognostic value of genetic lesions in CMML

### Interpretation

In CMML, EZH2 mutation was associated with worse overall survival in univariable analysis (HR 2.07; P=.023), but EZH2 was not among the mutations that retained independent prognostic value in the subsequent multivariable genetic model.
