## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraph beginning 'Chronic myelomonocytic leukemia (CMML) is a myeloid neoplasm'

**Secondary citation:** Bennett JM, Catovsky D, Daniel MT, et al. The chronic myeloid leukaemias: guidelines for distinguishing chronic granulocytic, atypical chronic myeloid, and chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia. Br J Haematol. 1994;87(4):746-754.

### Interpretation

According to the FAB classification, chronic myelomonocytic leukemia is classified as myelodysplastic CMML when the leukocyte count is ≤13 × 10^9/L and myeloproliferative CMML when it is >13 × 10^9/L.

## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, NRAS, RUNX1, SETBP1

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Prognostic value of genetic lesions in CMML, multivariable Cox regression paragraph; Results, Development of a clinical/molecular prognostic scoring system in CMML

### Interpretation

According to the CMML-specific genetic score developed as part of the CPSS-Mol framework, mutations in ASXL1, NRAS, RUNX1, and SETBP1 are independently associated with inferior overall survival in chronic myelomonocytic leukemia.

## elena-2016-blood-128-1408-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** EZH2

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Prognostic value of genetic lesions in CMML, univariable analysis and multivariable Cox regression paragraphs

### Interpretation

In chronic myelomonocytic leukemia, EZH2 mutation is associated with inferior overall survival in univariable analysis, but was not retained as an independent prognostic factor in multivariable analysis.
