# Kessler MD, Damask A, O’Keeffe S, Banerjee N, Li D, Watanabe K, et al. Common and rare variant associations with clonal haematopoiesis phenotypes. Nature. 2022;612(7939):301-309.

**Publication key:** `kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics`

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** CHEK2

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Genetic association with CHIP carrier status

### Interpretation

A rare germline CHEK2 frameshift variant was associated with increased CHIP risk, remained associated after conditioning on common-variant signals, and replicated in the GHS; this supports a germline CHEK2 predisposition signal for CHIP.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0005

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** LY75

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Analyses of individual CHIP gene mutations

### Interpretation

Two germline LY75 missense variants were associated with reduced DNMT3A-CHIP risk: rs78446341 (P1247L; OR 0.78, 95% CI 0.72–0.84), and rare rs147820690 (G525E), which associated with reduced risk at close to genome-wide significance (OR 0.48, 95% CI 0.36–0.63); the rarer signal remained associated after conditioning on the common signal, and the LY75 signals replicated in GHS.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0006

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TCL1A

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Analyses of individual CHIP gene mutations

### Interpretation

Germline TCL1A-locus lead SNPs had opposing CHIP-subtype effects: increased risk of DNMT3A CHIP (OR 1.14, 95% CI 1.11–1.17) but reduced risk of TET2 CHIP (OR 0.75, 95% CI 0.71–0.80) and ASXL1 CHIP (OR 0.70, 95% CI 0.65–0.76), with other analysed subtypes also directionally protective.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** PARP1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Analyses of individual CHIP gene mutations

**Secondary citation:** Gotoh N et al. PARP1 V762A polymorphism affects the prognosis of myelodysplastic syndromes. Eurr. J. Haematol. 2020;104:526–537.

### Interpretation

The paper restates prior evidence that the PARP1 rs1136410-G (V762A) missense variant is associated with improved prognosis and survival in MDS; this is secondary evidence, not an outcome analysis performed in the Kessler cohorts.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0008

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Longitudinal disease risk among CHIP carriers

### Interpretation

Among UKB high-VAF CHIP carriers (VAF ≥0.10), TET2 CHIP was associated with increased subsequent cardiovascular-disease risk (HR 1.31, 95% CI 1.14–1.51) in adjusted Cox models; the analysis excluded participants with malignant blood cancer before sequencing.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0009

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** PPM1D

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Phenotypic associations with CHIP

### Interpretation

In UKB analyses restricted to CHIP VAF ≥10% and excluding prior blood cancer, PPM1D-CHIP carriers may have elevated risk of severe COVID-19 (OR 5.42, 95% CI 1.89–12.2) after adjustment for age, sex, smoking, BMI, type 2 diabetes, active malignancy and genetic principal components.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0011

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A, ASXL1

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Longitudinal disease risk among CHIP carriers; Extended Data Fig. 9

### Interpretation

DNMT3A- and ASXL1-CHIP carriers had elevated lung-cancer risk in both smokers and non-smokers; the CHIP–lung-cancer association replicated in GHS and was not explained solely by smoking status, with adjusted Cox modelling and exclusion of pre-existing malignant cancer.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0012

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0013

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** PARP1, TET2

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Discussion

### Interpretation

PARP1 inhibition is an investigational strategy proposed to antagonize CHIP clone expansion, supported by protective PARP1-locus associations and secondary preclinical evidence in TET2-mutant haematopoietic stem cells; the authors explicitly caution about haematologic toxicity/treatment-related malignancy and state that further research is required, including subtype-specific effects.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0015

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** IL6R

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Longitudinal disease risk among CHIP carriers; Discussion

### Interpretation

The IL6R rs2228145-C coding variant, used as a genetic proxy for IL-6 inhibition, did not confer CHIP-specific CVD protection in the full UKB cohort or GHS. Thus this study does not support inferring reduced CVD risk from that proxy, while explicitly leaving the effect of direct pharmacological IL-6 inhibition unresolved.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0016

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, PPM1D, TP53, JAK2, SRSF2, SF3B1, BRAF, CSF3R, ETNK1, GNAS, KRAS, GNB1, IDH2, MPL, NRAS, PHF6, PRPF8, CBL, CALR, RUNX1, SUZ12

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Methods — Calling CHIP; Discussion

### Interpretation

For this study, CHIP detection was restricted to variants in 23 recurrent CHIP-associated genes after somatic-calling/QC refinement. The authors caution that population-scale blood/exome calling can still misclassify high-VAF somatic variants as germline and true germline variants as somatic, so somatic-versus-germline assignment remains a limitation of this callset.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0017

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

The study reports that DNMT3A-linked CHIP is associated with subsequent development of myeloid but not lymphoid leukaemias; the abstract attributes this conclusion to the study’s longitudinal and Mendelian-randomization analyses.

## kessler-2022-large-biobank-genetics-C0018

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Discussion; Methods — Risk modelling among CHIP carriers

### Interpretation

DNMT3A CHIP was not associated with cardiovascular disease in this study; the CVD models were multivariable-adjusted and excluded participants with malignant blood cancer before sequencing, and Mendelian-randomization analysis likewise did not support a CHIP–CVD association.
