## d-hner-2022-blood-140-1345-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** PML, RARA, RUNX1, RUNX1T1, CBFB, MYH11, MLLT3, KMT2A, DEK, NUP214, GATA2, MECOM, PRDM16, RPN1, RBM15, MRTFA, NPM1, MLF1, NUP98, NSD1, ETV6, MNX1, KAT6A, CREBBP, PICALM, MLLT10, KMD5A, FUS, ERG, CBFA2T3, GLIS2, CEBPA, BCR, ABL1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Changes to blast thresholds defining AML

### Interpretation

AML can be diagnosed with ≥10% blasts in blood or bone marrow in the presence of recurrent genetic abnormalities, which include: PML::RARA; RUNX1::RUNX1T1; CBFB::MYH11; MLLT3::KMT2A or another KMT2A rearrangement; DEK::NUP214; GATA2/MECOM or another MECOM rearrangement; PRDM16::RPN1; RBM15::MRTFA; NPM1::MLF1; NUP98::NSD1; ETV6::MNX1; KAT6A::CREBBP; PICALM::MLLT10; NUP98::KMD5A or another NUP98 rearrangement; FUS::ERG; RUNX1::CBFA2T3; CBFA2T3::GLIS2; mutated NPM1; or an in-frame bZIP CEBPA mutation. AML with BCR::ABL1 remains an exception and requires ≥20% blasts.

## d-hner-2022-blood-140-1345-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53, ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, RUNX1, SF3B1, SRSF2, STAG2, U2AF1, ZRSR2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML classification hierarchy

### Interpretation

AML with recurrent genetic abnormalities takes precedence over all other AML categories. Among the remaining categories, AML with mutated TP53 takes precedence over AML with myelodysplasia-related gene mutations, which takes precedence over AML with myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic abnormalities. AML with myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic abnormalities is defined, in the absence of higher-priority AML-defining categories, by a qualifying complex karyotype or del(5q)/t(5q)/add(5q), −7/del(7q), +8, del(12p)/t(12p)/add(12p), i(17q), −17/add(17p)/del(17p), del(20q), and/or idic(X)(q13).
