**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** AML classification > General classification; Figure 1

### Interpretation

AML classification is hierarchical: AML with a recurrent genetic abnormality takes precedence; among remaining categories, TP53-mutated disease supersedes myelodysplasia-related gene-mutated disease, which supersedes myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic disease.
