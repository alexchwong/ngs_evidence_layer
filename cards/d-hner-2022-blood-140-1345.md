## d-hner-2022-blood-140-1345-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** PML, RARA, RUNX1, RUNX1T1, CBFB, MYH11, MLLT3, KMT2A, DEK, NUP214, GATA2, MECOM, NPM1, CEBPA, BCR, ABL1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML classification — Changes to the blast thresholds defining AML; Table 1; Figure 1

### Interpretation

Under the 2022 International Consensus Classification (ICC), PML::RARA, RUNX1::RUNX1T1, CBFB::MYH11, MLLT3::KMT2A, DEK::NUP214, inv(3)/t(3;3) involving GATA2 and MECOM, mutated NPM1, and in-frame bZIP CEBPA mutations are AML-defining recurrent abnormalities at ≥10% blasts; BCR::ABL1 requires ≥20% blasts because of overlap with chronic myeloid leukemia.

## d-hner-2022-blood-140-1345-C0006

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML, MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML classification — AML with mutated TP53; Table 1; Figure 1; Table 1; Figure 1; AML classification — remaining AML cases

### Interpretation

Under the 2022 ICC, when higher-priority recurrent AML-defining abnormalities are absent, a pathogenic somatic TP53 mutation with variant allele fraction ≥10%, with or without loss of the wild-type allele, defines TP53-mutated AML at ≥20% blasts; the corresponding 10%-19% blast category is MDS/AML.

## d-hner-2022-blood-140-1345-C0007

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53, ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, RUNX1, SF3B1, SRSF2, STAG2, U2AF1, ZRSR2

**Diseases:** AML, MDS, MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML classification — AML with myelodysplasia-related gene mutations; Table 1; Figure 1; Table 1; Figure 1; AML classification — remaining AML cases

### Interpretation

Under the 2022 ICC, when higher-priority recurrent AML-defining abnormalities and TP53-mutated disease are absent, pathogenic mutations in ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, RUNX1, SF3B1, SRSF2, STAG2, U2AF1, or ZRSR2 define AML with myelodysplasia-related gene mutations at ≥20% blasts, irrespective of prior MDS; the corresponding 10%-19% blast category is MDS/AML.

**Diseases:** AML, MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 1 footnote ** — AML with myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic abnormalities; Table 1; Figure 1; AML classification — remaining AML cases

### Interpretation

Under the 2022 ICC, when higher-priority AML-defining categories are absent, del(5q)/t(5q)/add(5q), monosomy 7/del(7q), trisomy 8, del(12p)/t(12p)/add(12p), i(17q), monosomy 17/add(17p)/del(17p), del(20q), idic(X)(q13), or a complex karyotype of ≥3 unrelated chromosome abnormalities (excluding hyperdiploid karyotypes with ≥3 trisomies/polysomies and no structural abnormality) defines AML with myelodysplasia-related cytogenetic abnormality at ≥20% blasts and MDS/AML at 10%-19% blasts.
