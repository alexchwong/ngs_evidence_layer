# Passamonti F, Giorgino T, Mora B, Guglielmelli P, Rumi E, Maffioli M, et al. A clinical-molecular prognostic model to predict survival in patients with post polycythemia vera and post essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis. Leukemia. 2017;31(12):2726-2731.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** JAK2, MPL, CALR

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, MYSEC project genotype-phenotype associations paragraph

### Interpretation

Among patients with post-PV/post-ET MF, JAK2-mutated post-ET MF and triple-negative disease lacking JAK2, MPL and CALR mutations were reported to have a higher incidence of blast phase than CALR-mutated disease.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** JAK2, MPL, CALR

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Analysis of survival and identification of risk factors; Discussion, driver mutation paragraph

### Interpretation

In post-PV/post-ET MF, CALR-unmutated genotypes—JAK2-mutated, MPL-mutated, or triple-negative for JAK2, MPL and CALR—are associated with worse survival than CALR-mutated disease.
