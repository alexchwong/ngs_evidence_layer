## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR, JAK2, MPL

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Discussion, CALR prognostic paragraph

### Interpretation

In post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis, CALR-unmutated genotypes (JAK2-mutated, MPL-mutated, or triple-negative) were associated with worse survival in multivariable analysis.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Figure 2 legend

### Interpretation

MYSEC-PM, which incorporates CALR-unmutated genotype as a prognostic factor, stratified post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis into low-, intermediate-1-, intermediate-2-, and high-risk groups with median survival not reached, 9.3 years, 4.5 years, and 2.0 years, respectively.
