# Passamonti F, Giorgino T, Mora B, Guglielmelli P, Rumi E, Maffioli M, et al. A clinical-molecular prognostic model to predict survival in patients with post polycythemia vera and post essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis. Leukemia. 2017;31(12):2726-2731.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Discussion, diagnosis of SMF and molecularly updated PV/ET diagnosis

### Interpretation

For post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis, the paper describes diagnosis as requiring an antecedent WHO-based PV or ET diagnosis including appropriate mutations plus bone-marrow fibrosis above grade 1, and states that JAK2, CALR and MPL phenotype-driver mutation annotation provides a molecularly updated diagnosis of the antecedent PV/ET and consequently secondary myelofibrosis.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, analysis of survival and Table 3 multivariable model

### Interpretation

In 685 molecularly annotated patients with post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis, JAK2-mutated, MPL-mutated and triple-negative genotypes were grouped as CALR-unmutated; CALR-unmutated genotype independently predicted inferior survival (HR 2.6, 95% CI 1.2–5.3; P=0.001) and was assigned 2 points in MYSEC-PM.

## passamonti-2017-mysec-pm-secondary-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, prior MYSEC genotype-phenotype findings

**Secondary citation:** Passamonti F, Mora B, Giorgino T, Guglielmelli P, Cazzola M, Maffioli M, et al. Driver mutations' effect in secondary myelofibrosis: an international multicenter study based on 781 patients. Leukemia. 2016.

### Interpretation

In the previously reported 685-patient MYSEC secondary-myelofibrosis cohort, blast-phase incidence was higher in JAK2-mutated post-ET myelofibrosis and triple-negative patients (without JAK2, MPL or CALR mutations) than in CALR-mutated patients.
