## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** CLONAL HAEMATOPOIESIS — CHIP definition

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, clonal haematopoiesis is recognized as a precursor myeloid disease state, and CHIP is defined as clonal haematopoiesis with somatic mutations in myeloid malignancy-associated genes detected in blood or bone marrow at VAF ≥2% (≥4% for X-linked gene mutations in males), in the absence of a diagnosed haematologic disorder or unexplained cytopenia.

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** CLONAL HAEMATOPOIESIS — harmonized cytopenia thresholds

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, cytopenia thresholds harmonized across CCUS, MDS and MDS/MPN are haemoglobin <13 g/dL in males or <12 g/dL in females, absolute neutrophil count <1.8 × 10^9/L, and platelets <150 × 10^9/L.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0005

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS — CML phase classification

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, CML is classified into chronic and blast phases; accelerated phase is omitted. Blast phase is defined by ≥20% myeloid blasts in blood or bone marrow, extramedullary proliferation of blasts, or increased lymphoblasts in blood or marrow. The optimal lymphoblast cutoff and significance of low-level B-lymphoblasts remain uncertain.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0018

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS — CNL definition

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, chronic neutrophilic leukaemia is a BCR::ABL1-negative MPN characterized by sustained peripheral-blood neutrophilia with WBC ≥25 × 10^9/L and ≥80% segmented neutrophils and bands, bone-marrow hypercellularity due to neutrophilic granulocyte proliferation, and hepatosplenomegaly.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0019

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CEL

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS — CEL definition

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, chronic eosinophilic leukaemia is defined by sustained clonal proliferation of morphologically abnormal eosinophils and eosinophil precursors producing persistent hypereosinophilia in blood and marrow. Sustained hypereosinophilia requires ≥4 weeks; both clonality and abnormal bone-marrow morphology are required; increased blasts no longer substitute for clonality; and the former “not otherwise specified” qualifier is omitted.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0035

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC NEOPLASMS — core definition and dysplasia threshold

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, the term “myelodysplastic neoplasms,” abbreviated MDS, replaces “myelodysplastic syndromes.” MDS is defined as a clonal haematopoietic neoplasm with cytopenia(s) and morphologic dysplasia, with a recommended dysplasia threshold of ≥10% in a lineage.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0036

**Genes:** SF3B1, TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC NEOPLASMS — MDS-5q Table 3

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, MDS with low blasts and isolated 5q deletion requires <5% bone-marrow blasts and <2% peripheral-blood blasts, with 5q deletion alone or with one additional abnormality other than monosomy 7 or 7q deletion. **Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC NEOPLASMS — MDS-SF3B1 Table 3

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, MDS with low blasts and SF3B1 mutation is a genetically defined MDS type in which 5q deletion, monosomy 7 and complex karyotype are exclusionary. ## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0039

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC NEOPLASMS — MDS-biTP53 Table 3

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, MDS with biallelic TP53 inactivation requires <20% blasts in blood and marrow plus either ≥2 TP53 mutations or one TP53 mutation with TP53 copy-number loss or copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity. ## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0054

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** BCR, ABL1, CEBPA

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC NEOPLASMS — MDS/AML blast boundary

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, a 20% blast cutoff is retained to delineate MDS from AML, but most AML types with defining genetic abnormalities may be diagnosed below 20% blasts. AML with BCR::ABL1 and AML with CEBPA mutation remain exceptions requiring ≥20% blasts. ## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0059

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC/MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS — CMML prerequisite monocytosis

### Interpretation

According to WHO 5th Edition 2022, CMML requires all four prerequisite criteria: persistent absolute peripheral-blood monocytosis ≥0.5 × 10^9/L and relative monocytosis ≥10%; <20% blasts/blast equivalents in blood and marrow; absence of diagnostic criteria for CML or another MPN; and absence of a myeloid/lymphoid neoplasm with a tyrosine-kinase fusion. Supporting criteria are dysplasia in ≥1 myeloid lineage, with ≥10% of cells affected; an acquired clonal cytogenetic or molecular abnormality; and abnormal monocyte partitioning with >94% classical monocytes in the absence of active autoimmune/systemic inflammatory disease. With monocytosis ≥1 × 10^9/L, at least one supporting criterion is required; with monocytosis ≥0.5 but <1 × 10^9/L, both dysplasia and clonality are required. A prior MPN excludes CMML; MPN-type marrow features and/or a high burden of JAK2, CALR or MPL mutations support MPN with monocytosis rather than CMML.

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MYELODYSPLASTIC/MYELOPROLIFERATIVE NEOPLASMS — CMML WBC subtypes

### Interpretation

### Interpretation

### Interpretation
