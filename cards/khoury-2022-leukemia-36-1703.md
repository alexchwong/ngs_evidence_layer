# Khoury JD, Solary E, Abla O, Akkari Y, Alaggio R, Apperley JF, et al. The 5th edition of the World Health Organization Classification of Haematolymphoid Tumours: Myeloid and Histiocytic/Dendritic Neoplasms. Leukemia. 2022;36(7):1703-1719.

**Publication key:** `khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703`

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** BCR, ABL1

**Diseases:** CML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms — CML definition

### Interpretation

CML is defined by the BCR::ABL1 fusion resulting from t(9;22)(q34;q11).

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** PV

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** BCR::ABL1-negative MPN — PV major diagnostic criteria

### Interpretation

For polycythaemia vera, major diagnostic criteria include elevated haemoglobin and/or haematocrit, marrow panmyelosis with pleomorphic mature megakaryocytes, and JAK2 p.V617F or a JAK2 exon 12 mutation.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, SRSF2, U2AF1, ASXL1

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** BCR::ABL1-negative MPN — additional mutations and PMF prognosis

### Interpretation

In primary myelofibrosis, EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, SRSF2, U2AF1 and ASXL1 mutations are associated with poorer prognostic risk.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0004

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KMT2A

**Diseases:** JMML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** JMML — updated diagnostic criteria

### Interpretation

JMML diagnostic updates include exclusion of KMT2A rearrangements and greater emphasis on molecular studies demonstrating RAS-pathway activation.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** PTPN11, NF1, CBL

**Diseases:** JMML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** JMML — genotype and clinical behaviour

### Interpretation

In JMML, somatic PTPN11-mutated cases and cases associated with germline NF1 pathogenic variants are described as the most aggressive types, whereas some cases associated with pathogenic germline CBL variants may undergo spontaneous remission.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0006

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Mastocytosis — revised minor diagnostic criteria

### Interpretation

For systemic mastocytosis, any KIT mutation causing ligand-independent activation is accepted as a minor diagnostic criterion.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Mastocytosis — revised B-finding

### Interpretation

In systemic mastocytosis, KIT p.D816V with VAF ≥10% in bone-marrow cells or peripheral-blood leukocytes qualifies as a B-finding.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0008

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MDS with defining genetic abnormalities

### Interpretation

WHO-5 recognizes MDS with low blasts and SF3B1 mutation and MDS with biallelic TP53 inactivation as genetically defined MDS types; MDS-biTP53 supersedes MDS-5q and MDS-SF3B1.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0009

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MDS-5q — diagnostic precedence

### Interpretation

An SF3B1 or non-multi-hit TP53 mutation does not by itself override a diagnosis of MDS with isolated 5q deletion.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0010

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MDS-SF3B1 — defining and alternative criteria

### Interpretation

MDS-SF3B1 is a distinct MDS type; for SF3B1-wild-type cases, ≥15% ring sideroblasts permits the alternative term MDS with low blasts and ring sideroblasts.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MDS-biTP53 — allelic-state criteria and detection

### Interpretation

For MDS, biallelic TP53 alteration may consist of multiple TP53 mutations or one mutation with deletion of the other allele; TP53 VAF ≥50% is only presumptive, not definitive, evidence of trans-allelic copy loss or copy-neutral LOH after excluding a constitutional TP53 variant.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0012

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** MDS-biTP53 — prognosis

### Interpretation

MDS-biTP53 is described as very high risk in IPSS-R, with over 90% of affected patients having complex, usually very complex, karyotypes.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0013

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** CMML, MPN

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 6 footnote b — MPN with monocytosis versus CMML

### Interpretation

A high burden of JAK2, CALR or MPL mutations, together with MPN marrow features, tends to support MPN with monocytosis rather than CMML; a documented MPN history excludes CMML.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0014

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** BCR, ABL1, CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML with defining genetic abnormalities — retained blast thresholds

### Interpretation

AML with BCR::ABL1 fusion and AML with CEBPA mutation require at least 20% blasts for diagnosis; the BCR::ABL1 threshold helps avoid overlap with CML.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0015

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KMT2A, MECOM, NUP98

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML with defining rearrangements — blast threshold and detection

### Interpretation

AML with KMT2A, MECOM or NUP98 rearrangement may be diagnosed with fewer than 20% blasts; these rearrangements, particularly NUP98, may be cryptic on conventional karyotyping.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0016

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML with NPM1 mutation — diagnosis

### Interpretation

AML with NPM1 mutation can be diagnosed irrespective of blast count, with judicious clinicopathologic correlation.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0017

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML with CEBPA mutation — definition

### Interpretation

AML with CEBPA mutation includes biallelic CEBPA mutations and single mutations in the basic leucine zipper region.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0018

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML with defining genetic abnormalities — RUNX1 exclusion

### Interpretation

RUNX1-mutated AML is not recognized as a distinct AML type because RUNX1 mutations overlap with a broad range of defining molecular features and lack sufficient specificity.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0019

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1, ZRSR2, ASXL1, EZH2, BCOR, STAG2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** AML-MR — mutation-based definition

### Interpretation

AML, myelodysplasia-related requires ≥20% blasts and either a qualifying cytogenetic or molecular abnormality and/or prior MDS or MDS/MPN; qualifying mutations include SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1, ZRSR2, ASXL1, EZH2, BCOR and STAG2.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0020

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML, MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Myeloid neoplasms post cytotoxic therapy — TP53 prognosis

### Interpretation

In post-cytotoxic-therapy AML or MDS, biallelic TP53 alterations—two or more TP53 mutations, or a TP53 mutation with 17p/TP53 deletion or copy-neutral LOH—are associated with generally worse outcomes.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0021

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** PPM1D

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Myeloid neoplasms post cytotoxic therapy — possible germline work-up

### Interpretation

In post-cytotoxic-therapy myeloid neoplasms, PPM1D and DNA-damage-response gene mutations may require additional germline-predisposition work-up.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0022

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** germline predisposition syndrome

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloid neoplasms associated with germline predisposition — diagnostic framework

### Interpretation

The germline-predisposition framework couples the myeloid disease phenotype to the predisposing germline genotype; genetic counselling and family-history evaluation are expected components of the diagnostic evaluation.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0023

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** PDGFRA, FIP1L1, PDGFRB, ETV6

**Diseases:** myeloid/lymphoid neoplasm with eosinophilia and TK fusion

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MLN-TK — common defining rearrangements

### Interpretation

MLN-TK with PDGFRA rearrangement is most often caused by a cytogenetically cryptic 4q12 deletion producing FIP1L1::PDGFRA; PDGFRB rearrangement most commonly produces ETV6::PDGFRB.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0024

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, FLT3, ETV6, ABL1

**Diseases:** myeloid/lymphoid neoplasm with eosinophilia and TK fusion

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MLN-TK — JAK2, FLT3 and ETV6::ABL1

### Interpretation

MLN-TK with JAK2 rearrangement is recognized as a distinct type; FLT3 fusions are rare and ETV6::ABL1 MLN-TK should be separated from ETV6::ABL1 B-ALL.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0025

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** PDGFRA, PDGFRB, FGFR1, JAK2, FLT3, ETV6, ABL1

**Diseases:** myeloid/lymphoid neoplasm with eosinophilia and TK fusion

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** MLN-TK — TKI sensitivity

### Interpretation

The natural history of MLN-TK with PDGFRA or PDGFRB rearrangement has been dramatically altered by TKI therapy, particularly imatinib; FGFR1, JAK2 and FLT3 fusions and ETV6::ABL1 have more variable sensitivity to newer-generation TKIs.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0026

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** BRAF, ARAF, MAP2K1, NRAS, KRAS

**Diseases:** histiocytic/dendritic neoplasm

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Histiocytic neoplasms — MAPK alterations and targeted therapy

### Interpretation

In histiocytic neoplasms, MAPK-pathway alterations involving BRAF, ARAF, MAP2K1, NRAS or KRAS may provide targets for BRAF- or MEK-inhibitor therapy.

## khoury-2022-leukemia-36-1703-C0027

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** ALK, KIF5B

**Diseases:** histiocytic/dendritic neoplasm

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** ALK-positive histiocytosis — defining fusion and treatment response

### Interpretation

ALK-positive histiocytosis is unified by ALK translocation, most commonly KIF5B::ALK, and shows a remarkable response to ALK-inhibitor therapy.
