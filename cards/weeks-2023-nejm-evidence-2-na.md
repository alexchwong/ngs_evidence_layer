# Weeks LD, Niroula A, Neuberg D, Wong W, Lindsley RC, Luskin MR, et al. Prediction of Risk for Myeloid Malignancy in Clonal Hematopoiesis. NEJM Evidence. 2023 May;2(5):10.1056/EVIDoa2200310.

**Publication key:** `weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na`

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, SF3B1, ZRSR2, IDH1, IDH2, FLT3, RUNX1, JAK2

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Figure 1 legend (Panels G–H) and Figure 1H

### Interpretation

In U.K. Biobank participants with CHIP/CCUS, mutations in SRSF2, SF3B1, ZRSR2, IDH1, IDH2, FLT3, RUNX1, or JAK2 constituted the recursive-partitioning high-risk mutation feature, which independently predicted incident myeloid neoplasm after adjustment for sex, cancer history, and smoking history (hazard ratio 4.72; 95% CI, 3.54–6.29).

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Figure 1 legend (Panels G–H) and Figure 1H

### Interpretation

In U.K. Biobank participants with CHIP/CCUS, a single DNMT3A mutation was an independently lower-risk feature for incident myeloid neoplasm after adjustment for sex, cancer history, and smoking history (hazard ratio 0.578; 95% CI, 0.402–0.831).

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — TP53 inclusion in the CHRS high-risk mutation category

### Interpretation

TP53 was empirically included in the CHRS high-risk mutation category, but its inclusion did not significantly alter model performance; risk among TP53-mutant CHIP/CCUS was considered heterogeneous and potentially influenced by chemotherapy selection pressure, clone size, allelic state, and clonal complexity.

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0004

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** U2AF1

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — U2AF1 reference-genome calling limitation

### Interpretation

U2AF1 could not be evaluated reliably in this CHIP/CCUS analysis because an erroneous duplication in the hg38 reference genome precluded reliable variant calls; datasets with reliable U2AF1 calling are required before confirming that U2AF1-mutant clonal hematopoiesis is high risk.
