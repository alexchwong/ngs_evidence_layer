## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction

### Interpretation

Clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP) is defined as clonal hematopoiesis with somatic mutations detectable at a variant allele fraction (VAF) of 2% or greater in the absence of a diagnosed blood disorder or cytopenia.

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction; CHIP AND CCUS DESIGNATIONS

### Interpretation

Clonal cytopenia of undetermined significance (CCUS) is defined as clonal hematopoiesis with somatic mutations at a variant allele fraction (VAF) of 2% or greater in the presence of unexplained, persistent cytopenia.

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, SF3B1, ZRSR2, JAK2, RUNX1, IDH2

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** GENOTYPE-SPECIFIC RISK OF INCIDENT MN; Discussion

### Interpretation

According to the CHRS framework, mutations in SRSF2, SF3B1, ZRSR2, JAK2, RUNX1, and IDH2 are high-risk mutations for progression of CHIP or CCUS to myeloid neoplasm.

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** GENOTYPE-SPECIFIC RISK OF INCIDENT MN; Discussion

### Interpretation

Although the TP53-related mutation group was not associated with incident myeloid neoplasm in this cohort, the CHRS empirically treats TP53 mutation as high risk in CHIP/CCUS based on prior evidence and concern about underestimating risk.

DNMT3A-mutant CHIP/CCUS is associated with higher risk of myeloid neoplasm than individuals without CHIP/CCUS.

## weeks-2023-nejm-evidence-2-na-C0016

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Discussion

### Interpretation

In TP53-mutant CHIP/CCUS, myeloid-neoplasm risk is probably heterogeneous and may be influenced by clonal selection pressures such as chemotherapy, clone size, allelic state, and clonal complexity.
