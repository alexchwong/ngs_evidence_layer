## grief-2012-gata-cebpa-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** GATA2, CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

In cytogenetically normal AML, GATA2 ZF1 mutations were detected in 13 of 33 biallelic CEBPA-mutated cases and in none of 38 monoallelic CEBPA-mutated or 89 CEBPA-wild-type cases; the authors concluded that these findings support a genetically distinct subgroup of CN-AML.

## grief-2012-gata-cebpa-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** GATA2, CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results — GATA2 ZF1 mutations and clinical characteristics

### Interpretation

In biallelic CEBPA-mutated cytogenetically normal AML, additional GATA2 mutations did not negatively affect the favorable overall or event-free survival in the 26-patient survival cohort (10 GATA2-mutated; OS P = .274; EFS P = .185).
