## frebourg-2020-tp53-germline-C0013

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Recommendations > Cancer patients who should be tested for germline disease-causing TP53 > Recommendation 1, Familial presentation

### Interpretation

According to the ERN GENTURIS modified Chompret criteria, germline TP53 testing is recommended when a patient has a TP53 core tumour before age 46 years and at least one first- or second-degree relative has a TP53 core tumour before age 56 years.

**Locator:** Introduction > From Li–Fraumeni syndrome to heritable TP53-related cancers, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

Li–Fraumeni syndrome associated with germline TP53 alterations is characterised by strong familial aggregation of cancers, early-onset tumours, and a wide tumour spectrum.

## frebourg-2020-tp53-germline-C0039

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction > From Li–Fraumeni syndrome to heritable TP53-related cancers, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

The core cancer spectrum of TP53-associated Li–Fraumeni syndrome comprises soft-tissue sarcoma, osteosarcoma, adrenocortical carcinoma, central nervous system tumours, and very early-onset female breast cancer occurring before age 31 years.
