## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Methods — Genomic Characterization; Results — Feature Encoding for IPSS-M Model Development

### Interpretation

In the cohort used to develop IPSS-M, TP53 was encoded according to monoallelic versus multihit status. TP53 multihit was defined as multiple TP53 mutations or a TP53 mutation accompanied by loss of heterozygosity through chromosomal deletion or copy-neutral LOH.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53, FLT3, KMT2A

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Association Between Gene Mutations and Clinical End Points

### Interpretation

In MDS, TP53 multihit—but not monoallelic TP53 mutation—together with FLT3 mutations and KMT2A/MLL partial tandem duplication were the strongest adverse molecular predictors; FLT3 and MLL partial tandem duplication had the strongest associations with AML transformation.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, NRAS, RUNX1, STAG2, U2AF1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Association Between Gene Mutations and Clinical End Points

### Interpretation

In MDS, mutations in ASXL1, BCOR, EZH2, NRAS, RUNX1, STAG2, and U2AF1 were independently associated with adverse leukemia-free survival, overall survival, and AML-transformation outcomes after adjustment for confounders and IPSS-R score.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Association Between Gene Mutations and Clinical End Points

### Interpretation

In MDS, DDX41 mutation was associated with higher marrow blasts and AML-transformation risk but favorable overall survival, particularly in MDS with excess blasts.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0005

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Association Between Gene Mutations and Clinical End Points

### Interpretation

In DDX41-mutated MDS, the observed clonal/subclonal architecture was suggestive of a germline variant followed by a somatic second hit; the study classified 87% of DDX41-mutated patients as carrying a likely germline variant, but this was not constitutional confirmation.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, BCOR, BCORL1, NRAS, RUNX1, SRSF2, STAG2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Association Between Gene Mutations and Clinical End Points

### Interpretation

In MDS, favorable outcomes associated with SF3B1 mutation were confined to the SF3B1a group, defined as SF3B1 mutation without isolated del(5q) or comutation with BCOR, BCORL1, NRAS, RUNX1, SRSF2, or STAG2. The favorable association was not observed in the SF3B1-5q or SF3B1b groups, including in low-blast disease.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, CBL, DNMT3A, ETV6, EZH2, FLT3, IDH2, KRAS, KMT2A, NPM1, NRAS, RUNX1, SF3B1, SRSF2, TP53, U2AF1, BCOR, BCORL1, CEBPA, ETNK1, GATA2, GNB1, IDH1, NF1, PHF6, PPM1D, PRPF8, PTPN11, SETBP1, STAG2, WT1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Feature Selection for IPSS-M Model Development

### Interpretation

For MDS prognosis, IPSS-M assigns adverse main-effect weights to TP53 multihit, KMT2A/MLL partial tandem duplication, FLT3 internal tandem duplication or tyrosine-kinase-domain mutation, SF3B1-5q, NPM1, RUNX1, NRAS, ETV6, IDH2, CBL, EZH2, U2AF1, SRSF2, DNMT3A, ASXL1, and KRAS. SF3B1a is the sole favorable main-effect variable. The model also counts mutations in the residual genes BCOR, BCORL1, CEBPA, ETNK1, GATA2, GNB1, IDH1, NF1, PHF6, PPM1D, PRPF8, PTPN11, SETBP1, STAG2, and WT1. These residual genes were selected because they were not already individually modeled, occurred in more than 1% of the cohort, and had adverse effects of similar magnitude in univariable analysis. One residual-gene mutation therefore adds an adverse prognostic contribution, and two or more add a larger contribution; the model caps the residual count at two, so three or more residual-gene mutations receive the same residual weight as two. The residual count contributes to, but does not by itself determine, the patient's overall IPSS-M risk category.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0008

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** KMT2A, FLT3

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Restratification of Patients from IPSS-R to IPSS-M

### Interpretation

In MDS, 51 of 58 patients with KMT2A/MLL partial tandem duplication and 22 of 25 patients with FLT3 mutation—88% in each group—were upstaged from IPSS-R by IPSS-M.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0009

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Relation Between IPSS-M and Treatment

### Interpretation

In a correlative analysis of treated patients with MDS, TP53 multihit remained the strongest predictor of worse overall survival after hypomethylating agents, stem-cell transplantation, or lenalidomide.

## bernard-2022-nejm-evidence-1-na-C0010

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Relation Between IPSS-M and Treatment

### Interpretation

In MDS, DDX41 mutation—although not selected for inclusion in IPSS-M—was associated with favorable overall survival after hypomethylating-agent treatment despite its association with AML transformation. The apparent specificity of improved outcomes with hypomethylating agents requires investigation in randomized studies.
