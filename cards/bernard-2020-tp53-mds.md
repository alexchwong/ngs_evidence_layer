# Bernard E, Nannya Y, Hasserjian RP, Devlin SM, Tuechler H, Medina-Martinez JS, et al. Implications of TP53 allelic state for genome stability, clinical presentation and outcomes in myelodysplastic syndromes. Nature Medicine. 2020;26(10):1549-1556.

**Publication key:** `bernard-2020-tp53-mds`

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0001

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Title; main text, TP53 mutant subgroup definitions and allelic-state assessment (lines 25–34)

### Interpretation

In MDS, accurate TP53 allelic-state assignment requires assessment of allelic imbalance/LOH in addition to mutation count: the study defined monoallelic TP53 as a single mutation with a residual wild-type copy and multi-hit TP53 as at least two hits (multiple mutations, or mutation(s) with deletion or cnLOH), and showed that VAF alone is insufficient because one-quarter of cnLOH-positive patients would have been misassigned as monoallelic on the basis of VAF.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Main text, clinical outcomes and multivariable models (line 44)

### Interpretation

In MDS, multi-hit TP53 was an independent adverse prognostic factor after adjustment for age, cytogenetic risk and established predictive features, predicting death (HR 2.04, 95% CI 1.6–2.6; P=5×10−8) and AML transformation (HR 2.9, 95% CI 1.8–4.7; P=7×10−6); monoallelic TP53, considered as a group, was not different from TP53 wild type in these multivariable analyses. Multi-hit TP53 also identified poor survival across IPSS-R strata.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Title; main text, co-mutation burden and survival (line 165)

### Interpretation

In MDS with monoallelic TP53, prognosis varied with the number of co-occurring driver mutations: 5-year survival was 81% with no other identifiable mutation, 36% with one or two, 26% with three or four, and 8% with more than five. By contrast, multi-hit TP53 had uniformly poor 5-year survival (<6%) irrespective of additional mutation burden, supporting that monoallelic TP53 itself was not independently predictive of adverse risk.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Main text, VAF-stratified TP53 outcomes (lines 167–169)

### Interpretation

In MDS, an optimal cut-point analysis found that monoallelic TP53 with VAF >22% was associated with increased risk of death versus TP53 wild type (HR 2.2, 95% CI 1.5–3.2; P=0.0001), whereas monoallelic TP53 with VAF ≤22% had similar overall survival to wild type; the authors cautioned that a second TP53 hit may have been missed in some high-VAF monoallelic cases. Multi-hit TP53 remained very high risk across VAF ranges, including VAF ≤11%, and identified very-high-risk patients independently of TP53 VAF.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Main text, therapy-related MDS outcomes (lines 622–624)

### Interpretation

In therapy-related MDS, outcome differences by TP53 allelic state reiterated those seen in de novo MDS; among TP53-mutant therapy-related MDS, monoallelic patients had a lower risk of death than multi-hit patients (HR 0.39, 95% CI 0.15–1.0; P=0.05).

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0006

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Main text, serial MDS-to-AML progression cohort (lines 626–628)

### Interpretation

In an independent serial cohort of 12 patients with MDS who progressed to AML with a TP53 mutation, multiple TP53 hits were already present at MDS diagnosis in 7 patients and biallelic targeting emerged during progression in 3; the authors concluded that these data support assessing TP53 allelic state at diagnosis and for disease surveillance.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Title; main text, exploratory treatment-stratified survival analysis (line 632)

### Interpretation

In MDS, exploratory survival analyses stratified by TP53 allelic state showed evidence of longer survival for monoallelic versus multi-hit TP53 after hypomethylating-agent treatment and, among del(5q) patients, lenalidomide; the HSCT cohort was limited by small size and showed only a trend toward improved survival for monoallelic versus multi-hit TP53. The authors concluded that these observations highlight the importance of mapping TP53 allelic state in future correlative studies of treatment response.
