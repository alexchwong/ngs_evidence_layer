# Bernard E, Nannya Y, Hasserjian RP, Devlin SM, Tuechler H, Medina-Martinez JS, et al. Implications of TP53 allelic state for genome stability, clinical presentation and outcomes in myelodysplastic syndromes. Nature Medicine. 2020;26(10):1549-1556.

**Publication key:** `bernard-2020-tp53-mds`

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Main text and Fig. 1 legend defining TP53 allelic-state subgroups and monoallelic versus multi-hit states

### Interpretation

For TP53-mutated MDS, a single TP53 mutation with a retained wild-type copy represents a monoallelic state, whereas multiple TP53 mutations or TP53 mutation(s) with TP53 deletion or copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity represent multi-hit TP53, consistent with biallelic targeting.

## bernard-2020-tp53-mds-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Main text outcome, co-mutation and VAF analyses; Fig. 3; Extended Data Fig. 7

### Interpretation

In MDS, multi-hit TP53 is associated with inferior overall survival and independently identifies adverse-risk disease across IPSS-R strata; poor survival persists irrespective of TP53 VAF and the number of co-occurring driver mutations.
