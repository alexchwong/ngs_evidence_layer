# Bejar R, Lord A, Stevenson K, Bar-Natan M, Pérez-Ladaga A, Zaneveld J, et al. TET2 mutations predict response to hypomethylating agents in myelodysplastic syndrome patients. Blood. 2014;124(17):2705-2712.

**Publication key:** `tet2-asxl1-hma-response`

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates

### Interpretation

In the 213-patient MDS cohort treated with azacitidine or decitabine, TET2 mutations present in the major disease clone (mutations with VAF <10% treated as wild-type) were associated with a higher HMA response rate than TET2 wild-type (60% vs 43%; OR 1.99, P=.036; adjusted OR 1.98, P=.044).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates

### Interpretation

Among HMA-treated MDS patients, the subgroup with TET2 mutation and ASXL1 wild-type status had a higher overall response rate than all others; the association was strongest when mutations were required to have VAF >=10% (74% vs 44%; OR 3.65, P=.009).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** CBL

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates

### Interpretation

In this HMA-treated MDS cohort, when all detected VAFs were included, CBL mutations were associated with a lower response rate than CBL wild-type (14% vs 49%; OR 0.17, P=.023; adjusted OR 0.18, P=.027); this association was not significant when low-VAF mutations were treated as wild-type (20% vs 48%; OR 0.27, P=.10).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival

### Interpretation

Among the HMA-treated MDS patients with survival follow-up, TET2 mutation status was not associated with overall survival (P=.56), despite its association with treatment response.

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53, PTPN11

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival

### Interpretation

Among HMA-treated MDS patients with survival data, TP53 mutations were associated with lower overall survival (HR 2.01, P=.002; adjusted HR 1.91, P=.007), while PTPN11 mutations were associated with lower overall survival in unadjusted analysis (HR 3.26, P=.006) with an adjusted estimate of HR 2.47 (95% CI 0.98-6.26; P=.056).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival

### Interpretation

In this HMA-treated MDS cohort, TP53 mutation status refined the adverse prognostic significance of complex karyotype: median survival was 0.9 years for complex-karyotype/TP53-mutated patients, while complex-karyotype patients without detectable TP53 mutation had median survival 1.3 years and did not differ from patients without complex karyotypes (median 1.8 years; P=.28).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** RUNX1, ASXL1, EZH2, ETV6

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, survival findings

### Interpretation

In this HMA-treated MDS cohort, RUNX1, ASXL1, EZH2, and ETV6 mutations were not found to be prognostically adverse. The authors suggest that HMA treatment may partially abrogate the adverse prognostic impact of these lesions, but explicitly state that this finding requires validation.

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0008

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53, PTPN11

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract and Key Points

### Interpretation

In the 213-patient MDS cohort treated with azacitidine or decitabine, TP53 and PTPN11 mutations were associated with shorter overall survival but did not predict drug response; this study therefore does not support interpreting either mutation as a marker of HMA response resistance.
