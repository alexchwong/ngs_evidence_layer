# Bejar R, Lord A, Stevenson K, Bar-Natan M, Pérez-Ladaga A, Zaneveld J, et al. TET2 mutations predict response to hypomethylating agents in myelodysplastic syndrome patients. Blood. 2014;124(17):2705-2712.

**Publication key:** `tet2-asxl1-hma-response`

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Abstract; Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates

### Interpretation

In MDS treated with azacitidine or decitabine, TET2 mutations were associated with higher HMA response when variants with VAF <10% were treated as wild type (60% vs 43%; adjusted OR 1.98, P=.044).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates; Discussion

### Interpretation

Among HMA-treated MDS patients, the TET2-mutated/ASXL1-wild-type subgroup had a higher response rate when mutations were required to have VAF ≥10% (74% vs 44% for all other TET2/ASXL1 genotypes; OR 3.65, P=.009).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** CBL

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Clinical findings, variant allele fraction, and response rates

### Interpretation

In HMA-treated MDS, CBL mutations were associated with a lower response rate when all VAFs were included (14% vs 49%; adjusted OR 0.18, P=.027), but the association was not significant when low-VAF CBL mutations were treated as wild type (20% vs 48%; OR 0.27, P=.10).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival; Figure 4 legend

### Interpretation

Among HMA-treated MDS patients with survival data, TET2 mutation status was not associated with overall survival (P=.56), despite its association with treatment response.

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

### Interpretation

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** PTPN11

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival; Figure 4 legend

### Interpretation

Among HMA-treated MDS patients with survival data, PTPN11 mutation was associated with shorter overall survival in unadjusted analysis (HR 3.26, P=.006), but the adjusted association was attenuated and did not reach statistical significance (adjusted HR 2.47, P=.056).

## tet2-asxl1-hma-response-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Associations with overall survival

### Interpretation

In HMA-treated MDS, TP53 status further stratified patients with complex karyotype: median overall survival was 0.9 years with TP53 mutation, whereas complex-karyotype patients without detectable TP53 mutation had 1.3-year median survival, not significantly different from noncomplex karyotypes (1.8 years; P=.28). The authors concluded that TP53 status could better refine risk within complex-karyotype disease.
