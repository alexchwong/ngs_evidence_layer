## bolton-2020-chemo-ch-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Clinical implications of CH in patients with cancer; Fig. 4a

### Interpretation

Among 9,437 patients with cancer exposed to cancer therapy, CH-PD TP53 mutations were among the strongest gene-specific associations with therapy-related myeloid-neoplasm risk; tMN comprised MDS or AML, 75 tMN events occurred, CH was evaluated above a 2% VAF threshold, and the cause-specific Cox models were adjusted for age and sex and stratified by study center. A TP53-specific hazard ratio is not provided in the cited passage.

## bolton-2020-chemo-ch-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Clinical implications of CH in patients with cancer; Methods — Combined analysis for AML/MDS risk

### Interpretation

Among 9,437 patients with cancer exposed to cancer therapy, CH-PD mutations in the spliceosome genes SRSF2, U2AF1 and SF3B1 were among the strongest gene-specific associations with tMN risk, defined as MDS or AML after therapeutic radiation or cytotoxic therapy, in univariate cause-specific Cox analysis using a universal 2% minimum VAF cutoff, adjusted for age and sex and stratified by study site. SRSF2 was not covered by the MSK-IMPACT-341 panel; gene-specific hazard ratios are not provided in the cited passage.
