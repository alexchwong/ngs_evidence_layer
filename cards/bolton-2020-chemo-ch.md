# Bolton KL, Ptashkin RN, Gao T, Braunstein L, Devlin SM, Kelly D, et al. Cancer therapy shapes the fitness landscape of clonal hematopoiesis. Nature Genetics. 2020;52(11):1219-1226.

**Publication key:** `bolton-2020-chemo-ch`

## bolton-2020-chemo-ch-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53, PPM1D, CHEK2

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

In clonal hematopoiesis, radiation, platinum, and topoisomerase II inhibitor therapy preferentially selects TP53-, PPM1D-, and CHEK2-mutant DNA-damage-response clones; serial sampling showed DDR clones outcompete other clones under therapy.

## bolton-2020-chemo-ch-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Clinical implications of CH in patients with cancer; Fig. 4a; Supplementary Table 2

### Interpretation

Among cancer patients exposed to cancer therapy, TP53-mutated clonal hematopoiesis showed one of the strongest gene-level associations with subsequent therapy-related myeloid neoplasm risk.

## bolton-2020-chemo-ch-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, SF3B1

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Clinical implications of CH in patients with cancer; Fig. 4a; Supplementary Table 2

### Interpretation

Among cancer patients exposed to cancer therapy, clonal hematopoiesis with spliceosome-gene mutations in SRSF2, U2AF1, or SF3B1 was among the strongest gene-level associations with subsequent therapy-related myeloid neoplasm risk.
