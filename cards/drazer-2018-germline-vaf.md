# Drazer MW, Kadri S, Sukhanova M, Patil SA, West AH, Feurstein S, et al. Prognostic tumor sequencing panels frequently identify germ line variants associated with hereditary hematopoietic malignancies. Blood Advances. 2018;2(2):146-150.

**Publication key:** `drazer-2018-germline-vaf`

## drazer-2018-germline-vaf-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1, SRP72, TERT, TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Methods; Results; Discussion (VAF-based germline assessment and limitations)

### Interpretation

Among the 44 patients with germ line tissue available, 52 pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants in HHM-associated genes were assessed; among the 25 pathogenic variants with tumor VAF >0.4, 6 (24%) were germ line, and no pathogenic germ line variant in this tissue-available subset had VAF <0.4. Low tumor VAF should nevertheless not exclude germline testing because structural aberrations can skew VAF; the authors perform hereditary evaluation in patients at high risk for HHMs regardless of tumor VAF. The cohort was potentially enriched for HHMs, so the reported proportions may not generalize to broader populations.

## drazer-2018-germline-vaf-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41, GATA2, TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results; Discussion (gene-specific germline likelihood and prioritization)

### Interpretation

In this potentially HHM-enriched cohort, known Li-Fraumeni-associated TP53 variants and known pathogenic DDX41 or GATA2 variants were especially likely to be germline. The authors specifically recommend prioritizing DDX41 and GATA2 variants for germline evaluation when detected by tumor-only sequencing, and state that tumor-based panels should augment rather than replace comprehensive germline testing and counseling.

## drazer-2018-germline-vaf-C0003

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results; patient 17 case description

### Interpretation

In patient 17, tumor-only sequencing of a therapy-related myeloid neoplasm detected DDX41 p.Asp140Glyfs*2, described as a known pathogenic germline frameshift that had not been observed as a somatic mutation; cultured skin fibroblasts confirmed germline origin. Despite no family history of hematopoietic malignancy, the authors use this case to show that tumor-only sequencing may provide the initial evidence of an HHM in individuals with germline DDX41 mutations.
