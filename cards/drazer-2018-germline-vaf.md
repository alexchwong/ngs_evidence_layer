## drazer-2018-germline-vaf-C0004

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1, TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Discussion, paragraph 3

### Interpretation

For pathogenic ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1, or TP53 variants detected by tumor-only sequencing, VAF <0.4 makes germline origin less likely but should not be used to exclude germline testing because uniparental disomy, loss of heterozygosity, or primer-site variants can skew VAF.
