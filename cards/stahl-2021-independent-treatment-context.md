# Stahl M, Menghrajani K, Derkach A, Chan A, Xiao W, Glass J, et al. Clinical and molecular predictors of response and survival following venetoclax therapy in relapsed/refractory AML. Blood Advances. 2021;5(5):1552-1564.

**Publication key:** `stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context`

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1, IDH1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Molecular predictors of response

### Interpretation

In this retrospective cohort of 86 patients with relapsed/refractory AML treated with venetoclax combinations, NPM1 and IDH1 mutations were associated with significantly increased response rates in univariable analysis; after adjustment for co-occurrence of favorable prognostic mutations, only NPM1 remained associated with improved response to venetoclax therapy.

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Molecular predictors of response

### Interpretation

Among patients with relapsed/refractory AML carrying a DNMT3A mutation and treated with venetoclax combinations, prior HMA exposure was associated with lower response: ORR was 17% (2/12) with prior HMA therapy versus 67% (10/15) without prior HMA therapy (OR 0.1, 95% CI 0.02-0.65; P=.02), and this association persisted after accounting for number of prior therapy lines.

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular predictors of survival

### Interpretation

In the venetoclax-combination-treated relapsed/refractory AML cohort, DNMT3A mutation status significantly interacted with prior HMA exposure for overall survival (P=.048): DNMT3A-mutated patients without prior HMA exposure had median OS not reached (95% CI 10 months to not reached), whereas those with prior HMA exposure had median OS 5.3 months (95% CI 1.8 months to not reached).

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53, NRAS, KRAS, SF3B1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Multivariable model of clinical characteristics and molecular predictors associated with survival

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory AML treated with venetoclax combinations, TP53, NRAS/KRAS, and SF3B1 mutations remained independently associated with worse overall survival in multivariable analysis.

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** EZH2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Molecular predictors of survival; multivariable model

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory AML treated with venetoclax combinations, EZH2 mutation was associated with decreased overall survival in univariable analysis (HR 4.13, 95% CI 1.43-11.96; P=.01); in the multivariable model, the adverse association reached only borderline statistical significance.

## stahl-2021-independent-treatment-context-C0006

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** STAG2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular predictors of survival

### Interpretation

In the venetoclax-combination-treated relapsed/refractory AML cohort, STAG2 mutations were associated with improved overall survival in univariable analysis (HR 0.32, 95% CI 0.1-1.02; P=.04).
