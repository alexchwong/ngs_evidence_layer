# Knudsen TA, Skov V, Stevenson K, Werner L, Duke W, Laurore C, et al. Genomic profiling of a randomized trial of interferon-α vs hydroxyurea in MPN reveals mutation-specific responses. Blood Advances. 2022;6(7):2107-2119.

**Publication key:** `mpn-genomics-interferon-response`

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

Among JAK2-mutated patients with MPN treated with interferon-alpha, those achieving CHR at 24 months had a greater reduction in JAK2 VAF (median 0.29 to 0.07) than those not achieving CHR (median 0.27 to 0.14).

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** CALR

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results — Association between somatic mutations and complete CHR on serial sampling

### Interpretation

In CALR-mutated MPN treated with interferon-alpha, CALR VAF did not significantly decline at 24 months in either patients achieving CHR (median 0.17 to 0.13; P = .078) or those not achieving CHR (0.21 to 0.17; P = .066); only 18 CALR-positive interferon-treated patients were evaluable, limiting precision.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Discussion

### Interpretation

In MPN, treatment-emergent DNMT3A mutations were more common with interferon-alpha than hydroxyurea and were enriched among interferon-treated patients failing to achieve CHR; the study could not distinguish expansion of pre-existing DNMT3A-mutated clones from de novo induction by interferon, so this association should not be interpreted as proof that interferon caused the mutations.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion opening; Results — Association between somatic mutations and complete CHR on serial sampling

### Interpretation

In the DALIAH cohort of newly diagnosed MPN, baseline JAK2, JAK2-UPD, CALR, DNMT3A, TET2, or ASXL1 status was not associated with probability of CHR at 24 months, either overall or when stratified by hydroxyurea versus interferon-alpha; other concomitant mutations were too infrequent for analysis.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — treatment response

### Interpretation

Among JAK2-mutated MPN, JAK2 uniparental disomy (JAK2-UPD) was associated with a greater decrease in JAK2 VAF during interferon-alpha treatment than absence of JAK2-UPD; this differential pattern was not seen with hydroxyurea.
