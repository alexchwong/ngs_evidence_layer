# Knudsen TA, Skov V, Stevenson K, Werner L, Duke W, Laurore C, et al. Genomic profiling of a randomized trial of interferon-α vs hydroxyurea in MPN reveals mutation-specific responses. Blood Advances. 2022;6(7):2107-2119.

**Publication key:** `mpn-genomics-interferon-response`

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction: phenotypic driver mutations

### Interpretation

In MPN, absence of mutations in all three phenotypic driver genes JAK2, CALR, and MPL defines the molecular designation “triple-negative.”

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction: long-term interferon molecular responses

### Interpretation

In JAK2V617F-mutated MPN, long-term interferon-alpha has been associated with molecular remission and bone-marrow normalization; in a minority, these responses may persist after treatment discontinuation, an effect the paper states has not been observed with hydroxyurea.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results: Somatic mutations on serial sampling

### Interpretation

In serially sampled DALIAH patients with newly diagnosed MPN, every phenotypic driver mutation present at baseline remained detectable by NGS at 24 months after interferon-alpha or hydroxyurea treatment.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results: Somatic mutations on serial sampling

### Interpretation

In DALIAH patients with newly diagnosed MPN, interferon-alpha was associated with a larger 24-month reduction in JAK2-mutant allele burden than hydroxyurea: JAK2 VAF decreased in 94% versus 75% of patients, with a greater median absolute reduction (0.11 vs 0.05).

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0006

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** CALR

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results: Somatic mutations on serial sampling

### Interpretation

In DALIAH patients with newly diagnosed MPN, 24-month CALR-mutant allele-burden reduction did not significantly differ between interferon-alpha and hydroxyurea.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results: Somatic mutations on serial sampling

### Interpretation

Among JAK2-mutated DALIAH patients, those with JAK2-UPD had a greater JAK2 VAF reduction during interferon-alpha treatment than those without JAK2-UPD; this differential reduction was not observed with hydroxyurea.

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0015

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** MPN

**Locator:** Results: Somatic mutations on serial sampling

### Interpretation

Among DALIAH patients with newly diagnosed MPN who had treatment-emergent mutations, DNMT3A mutations were more common during interferon-alpha than hydroxyurea treatment (11/18 [61%] vs 3/14 [21%]; P=.046).

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0016

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results: Association between somatic mutations and complete CHR on serial sampling

### Interpretation

Among interferon-alpha-treated DALIAH patients with treatment-emergent mutations, emergent DNMT3A mutations were significantly enriched in patients failing to achieve complete clinicohematologic response compared with emergent non-DNMT3A mutations (89% vs 20%; P=.02).

## mpn-genomics-interferon-response-C0017

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Methods: NGS analysis; Discussion: JAK2-UPD assay interpretation

### Interpretation

In JAK2-mutated MPN, targeted NGS that incorporates informative chromosome 9p SNP coverage can determine JAK2 uniparental disomy (JAK2-UPD), enabling distinction between heterozygous and homozygous JAK2-mutated states; the authors note that this is particularly informative when JAK2 VAF is <50%.
