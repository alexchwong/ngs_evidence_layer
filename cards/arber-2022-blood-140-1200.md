**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; CML; Table 2

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, accelerated-phase CML is defined by any of the following: 10%-19% blasts in peripheral blood or bone marrow; peripheral-blood basophils >=20%; or a major-route additional clonal cytogenetic abnormality in Philadelphia-positive cells at diagnosis or acquired during treatment (second Philadelphia chromosome, trisomy 8, i(17q), trisomy 19, complex karyotype, or 3q26.2 abnormality).

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0004

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** PV

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; PV; Table 3

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, polycythemia vera requires either all 3 major criteria or the first 2 major criteria plus the minor criterion. Major criteria are: elevated hemoglobin (>16.5 g/dL in men or >16.0 g/dL in women), elevated hematocrit (>49% in men or >48% in women), or red-cell mass >25% above predicted; JAK2 V617F or JAK2 exon 12 mutation; and age-adjusted hypercellular marrow with trilineage proliferation (panmyelosis). The minor criterion is a subnormal serum erythropoietin level.

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, post-polycythemia vera myelofibrosis requires both required criteria—previous established polycythemia vera and bone-marrow fibrosis grade 2 or 3—plus at least 2 additional criteria: anemia or sustained loss of requirement for phlebotomy/cytoreductive treatment for erythrocytosis; leukoerythroblastosis; increased or newly palpable splenomegaly; or development of at least 2 of >10% weight loss in 6 months, night sweats, and unexplained fever >37.5°C.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0008

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** ABL1, BCR, CALR, JAK2, MPL

**Diseases:** ET

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; essential thrombocythemia; Table 4

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, essential thrombocythemia requires all 4 major criteria or the first 3 major criteria plus the minor criterion. Major criteria are platelet count >=450 x 10^9/L; characteristic megakaryocytic-lineage proliferation with enlarged mature hyperlobulated megakaryocytes and no significant granulocytic/erythroid proliferation or relevant fibrosis; exclusion of BCR::ABL1-positive CML, polycythemia vera, primary myelofibrosis, and other myeloid neoplasms; and JAK2, CALR, or MPL mutation. The minor criterion is another clonal marker or absence of reactive thrombocytosis.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** ABL1, BCR, CALR, JAK2, MPL

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; primary myelofibrosis; Table 5

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, pre-fibrotic/early primary myelofibrosis requires all 3 major criteria and at least 1 minor criterion confirmed on 2 consecutive determinations. Major criteria are characteristic megakaryocytic proliferation/atypia with marrow fibrosis <grade 2, increased age-adjusted cellularity, granulocytic proliferation and often decreased erythropoiesis; JAK2, CALR, or MPL mutation, another clonal marker, or absence of reactive reticulin fibrosis; and exclusion of BCR::ABL1-positive CML, polycythemia vera, essential thrombocythemia, MDS, and other myeloid neoplasms. Minor criteria are unexplained anemia, leukocytosis >=11 x 10^9/L, palpable splenomegaly, or elevated LDH.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0015

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** ABL1, BCR, CSF3R

**Diseases:** CNL

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; chronic neutrophilic leukemia; Table 6

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, chronic neutrophilic leukemia requires neutrophilic leukocytosis with segmented/band neutrophils >=80%, neutrophil precursors <10%, monocytes <10%, no significant dysgranulopoiesis, and hypercellular marrow with increased normally maturing neutrophil granulocytes; exclusion of BCR::ABL1-positive CML, polycythemia vera, essential thrombocythemia, primary myelofibrosis, and myeloid/lymphoid neoplasms with eosinophilia and TK fusions; and either CSF3R T618I/another activating CSF3R mutation or, if CSF3R is absent, persistent neutrophilia for >=3 months with splenomegaly and no reactive cause (with myeloid clonality demonstrated if a plasma-cell neoplasm is present). The WBC threshold is >=13 x 10^9/L with an activating CSF3R mutation and >=25 x 10^9/L without one; absence of CSF3R does not exclude CNL.

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, chronic eosinophilic leukemia, NOS requires all 6 criteria: peripheral-blood eosinophils >=1.5 x 10^9/L and >=10% of leukocytes; <20% blasts in blood and marrow without another AML-defining recurrent genetic abnormality; absence of BCR::ABL1 and other ABL1, PDGFRA, PDGFRB, FGFR1, JAK2, or FLT3 TK fusions; failure to meet criteria for another well-defined MPN, CMML, or systemic mastocytosis; characteristic marrow abnormalities or increased blasts (>=5% marrow and/or >=2% blood); and a clonal cytogenetic abnormality and/or somatic mutation. **Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Mastocytosis; Table 11

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, systemic mastocytosis is established by the major criterion—multifocal dense aggregates of >=15 tryptase- and/or CD117-positive mast cells in marrow and/or another extracutaneous organ—or, when the major criterion is absent, by at least 3 of 4 minor criteria: >25% spindle-shaped/atypical immature mast cells; mast-cell expression of CD25, CD2, and/or CD30; KIT D816V or another activating KIT mutation; and persistent serum tryptase >20 ng/mL (not counted in SM with an associated myeloid neoplasm). ## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0041

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** ABL1, BCR

**Diseases:** CMML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** MDS/MPN; CMML; Table 13

### Interpretation

According to the ICC, CMML requires persistent monocytosis (absolute monocytes >=0.5 x 10^9/L and >=10% of WBCs), cytopenia using MDS thresholds except that some early cases may have borderline/no cytopenia, <20% blasts including promonocytes in blood and marrow, marrow morphology consistent with CMML, and absence of BCR::ABL1 or TK-fusion myeloid/lymphoid neoplasms. Clonality is established by abnormal cytogenetics and/or a myeloid-neoplasm-associated mutation with allele frequency >=10%; without demonstrable clonality, monocytes must be >=1.0 x 10^9/L and >10% of WBCs together with increased blasts, morphologic dysplasia, or an abnormal CMML immunophenotype.
