## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, MPL, CALR

**Diseases:** PV, ET, PMF

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloproliferative neoplasms; paper.md line 101

### Interpretation

Highly sensitive testing for JAK2 V617F, JAK2 exon 12, MPL W515L/K, and CALR with a minimum sensitivity of 1% VAF supports diagnosis of PV, ET, or PMF and distinction of wild-type or triple-negative cases. In triple-negative cases, searching for noncanonical JAK2 mutations is encouraged; searching for noncanonical MPL mutations is encouraged in suspected ET or PMF.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0009

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2, CALR, MPL, SETBP1, ASXL1, CSF3R

**Diseases:** aCML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** aCML discussion; paper.md line 363

### Interpretation

When distinguishing aCML from advanced-stage MPN, absence of JAK2, CALR, and MPL driver mutations supports aCML, whereas a history of MPN or the presence of these mutations tends to exclude it. SETBP1 mutation, often with co-mutated ASXL1, supports aCML; a detected CSF3R mutation should prompt careful morphologic review for CNL.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/AML, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Myeloid neoplasms with mutated TP53 discussion; paper.md line 528

### Interpretation

ICC TP53-mutated myeloid-neoplasm classification is assigned across MDS, MDS/AML, and AML according to blast percentage; multihit TP53 is established by two distinct TP53 mutations with VAF at least 10%, or one TP53 mutation with 17p deletion, VAF above 50%, copy-neutral LOH at TP53, or—when LOH is unavailable—complex karyotype.

## arber-2022-blood-140-1200-C0012

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/AML, AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Myeloid neoplasms with mutated TP53 discussion; paper.md line 528

### Interpretation

TP53-mutated MDS, MDS/AML, and AML are grouped because of similarly aggressive behaviour across blast percentages; multihit TP53 cytopenic myeloid neoplasms have short survival, while monoallelic TP53 mutations in MDS are less adverse than multihit disease.
