# Baliakas P, Tesi B, Wartiovaara-Kautto U, Stray-Pedersen A, Friis LS, Dybedal I, et al. Nordic Guidelines for Germline Predisposition to Myeloid Neoplasms in Adults: Recommendations for Genetic Diagnosis, Clinical Management and Follow-up. HemaSphere. 2019;3(6):e321.

**Publication key:** `baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing`

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6, CEBPA

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Criteria B — somatic-panel variants suspected to be germline

### Interpretation

In patients with MDS or AML, RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6 or CEBPA variants detected on a somatic NGS panel at VAF 40%–60% (near-heterozygous) or >90% (near-homozygous) may suggest germline origin; the respective variant should be tested in extra-haematological tissue after consent rather than treating tumour-only VAF as confirmation.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Criteria B — DDX41 age and variant class

### Interpretation

DDX41-associated MDS or AML may present at older ages, with variants reported in patients aged 55–65 years; truncating DDX41 variants are usually of germline origin, so older age does not exclude germline predisposition.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0003

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2, SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Criterion C — chromosome 7 aberrations in young MDS/AML

### Interpretation

For patients diagnosed with MDS or AML before age 50 who do not fulfil criteria A or B, chromosome 7 loss abnormalities (including monosomy 7, del(7q), or other loss of 7q material) warrant referral for germline genetic counselling/testing; such abnormalities are particularly common in GATA2-, SAMD9- and SAMD9L-related disorders, including when family or personal history is not suggestive.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0004

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TERC, RUNX1

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Criteria A1 footnote — poor sibling donor stem-cell mobilization

### Interpretation

In a patient with MDS or AML diagnosed before age 50, poor stem-cell mobilization of a sibling candidate donor is among the hereditary-condition signs that support germline evaluation; this finding has been reported rarely in association with germline TERC and RUNX1 variants.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0005

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1, ANKRD26, ETV6

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Baseline surveillance — carrier blood-count/bleeding phenotype

### Interpretation

Asymptomatic carriers of RUNX1, ANKRD26 or ETV6 variants may have baseline thrombocytopenia, and a bleeding tendency may occur even without thrombocytopenia.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0006

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Surveillance — timing for asymptomatic DDX41 carriers

### Interpretation

The start of surveillance in asymptomatic mutation carriers should be individualized to the disorder and family; DDX41-associated MDS or AML presents in mid-to-late adulthood.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0007

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2, RUNX1

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Surveillance — complete blood counts

### Interpretation

For high-risk individuals with pathogenic GATA2 or RUNX1 variants, the working group recommends repeating the complete blood count every 6 months.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0008

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** GATA2, RUNX1

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Surveillance — peripheral-blood somatic mutation monitoring

### Interpretation

For hereditary predisposition related mainly to germline GATA2 or RUNX1 mutations, annual high-depth blood NGS for somatic myeloid mutations has potential surveillance value for MDS/AML risk. A new somatic mutation or persistent/steep VAF increase should prompt further investigation including bone marrow assessment, but clonal emergence alone should not trigger clinical action; gene, VAF, number of pathogenic variants and serial dynamics should be considered.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Considerations for allo-HSCT — germline CEBPA AML

### Interpretation

For AML associated with germline CEBPA, there is no absolute indication for allo-HSCT in first complete remission because long remissions can occur after conventional chemotherapy; despite a high risk of new leukemic clones, allo-HSCT may be considered later.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0010

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Considerations for allo-HSCT — germline DDX41 AML

### Interpretation

For AML with germline DDX41 mutations, the authors make no specific allo-HSCT recommendation because of insufficient data; the source also notes that these patients are often older.
