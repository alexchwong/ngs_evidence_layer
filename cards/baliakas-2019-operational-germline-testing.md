## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0019

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6, CEBPA

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Criteria for whom to test — criterion B

### Interpretation

In MDS or AML, a RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6, or CEBPA variant detected on a somatic NGS panel with VAF 40–60% or >90% can suggest possible germline origin.

## baliakas-2019-operational-germline-testing-C0020

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6, CEBPA

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Criteria for whom to test — criterion B

### Interpretation

When a RUNX1, GATA2, TP53, ETV6, or CEBPA variant detected on a somatic NGS panel is suspected to be germline in MDS/AML, confirmation in extra-hematological tissue is highly recommended after consent.
