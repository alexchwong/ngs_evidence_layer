# Döhner K, Thiede C, Jahn N, Panina E, Gambietz A, Larson RA, et al. Impact of NPM1/FLT3-ITD genotypes defined by the 2017 European LeukemiaNet in patients with acute myeloid leukemia. Blood. 2020;135(5):371-380.

**Publication key:** `dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction`

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Categorization of patients; Survival analysis by ELN risk groups; Outcome analysis by ELN risk group and treatment arm

### Interpretation

In the 318-patient RATIFY ELN biomarker cohort with FLT3-ITD AML, 2017 ELN risk categorization based on NPM1/FLT3-ITD genotype and applicable high-risk molecular/cytogenetic features separated overall survival: 5-year OS was 0.62, 0.43, and 0.33 for favorable-, intermediate-, and adverse-risk AML, respectively (P<.001), and the ELN classification remained a significant prognostic variable in the multivariable OS model.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — prognostic value of 2017 ELN categorization

### Interpretation

Among RATIFY patients with FLT3-ITD AML who could be fully categorized by 2017 ELN, NPM1-mutated/FLT3-ITD-low AML had favorable prognosis; 5-year OS was 62.5% across both treatment arms and 73.0% in the midostaurin arm.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — midostaurin effect across ELN risk groups; age limitation

### Interpretation

In this retrospective RATIFY analysis of patients aged 18-60 years with FLT3-ITD AML, midostaurin had a beneficial effect on overall survival across all three 2017 ELN risk groups; the effect appeared independent of FLT3-ITD allelic burden and was seen on mutational backgrounds with or without NPM1 mutation. The subgroup findings should not be automatically extrapolated to patients older than 60 years.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Discussion — allogeneic HCT and postremission strategy; age limitation

### Interpretation

In this retrospective RATIFY analysis of patients aged 18-60 years with FLT3-ITD AML, exploratory subgroup analyses found a strong beneficial effect of allogeneic HCT in CR1 only in the ELN adverse-risk group; the trial was not powered for definitive subgroup treatment comparisons. The authors further considered conventional consolidation plus midostaurin a postremission option for ELN favorable-risk FLT3-ITD AML, with allogeneic HCT potentially delayed until first relapse.

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Discussion — measurable residual disease monitoring in ELN favorable-risk FLT3-ITD AML

### Interpretation

For the ELN favorable-risk FLT3-ITD AML population discussed as candidates for conventional consolidation plus midostaurin, NPM1 mutation provides a solid target for measurable residual disease monitoring and can further refine prognostic assessment used to inform postremission therapy.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0007

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Patients and methods — Genetic analyses; Discussion — FLT3-ITD allelic-ratio assay

### Interpretation

In RATIFY, FLT3-ITD allelic ratio was measured semiquantitatively by PCR with capillary-electrophoresis fragment analysis; testing used triplicate measurements and cross-laboratory quality control. The authors note residual allelic-ratio variability, reduced by triplicate analysis, and a need for further assay harmonization and standardization.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0008

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1, RUNX1, ASXL1, TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Categorization of patients

### Interpretation

In the 2017 ELN categorization applied to this FLT3-ITD AML cohort, concurrent RUNX1, ASXL1, or TP53 mutations were treated as high-risk molecular markers: their absence was required for the specified NPM1-mutated/FLT3-ITD-high and NPM1-wild-type/FLT3-ITD-low intermediate-risk subgroups, whereas their presence contributed to adverse-risk assignment in those genotype contexts.
