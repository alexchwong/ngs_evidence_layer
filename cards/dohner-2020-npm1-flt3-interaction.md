## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, RUNX1, ASXL1, TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Patients, lines 60 and 64; Results, lines 123, 135, and 179; Discussion, line 223

### Interpretation

Among the 318 RATIFY patients aged 18-60 with FLT3-ITD AML for whom complete 2017 ELN genetic risk categorization using NPM1/FLT3-ITD status together with RUNX1, ASXL1, TP53 and cytogenetic risk features was possible, 5-year overall survival was 0.62 for favorable risk, 0.43 for intermediate risk, and 0.33 for adverse risk; the ELN classification remained a significant prognostic variable in multivariable analysis.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0008

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Genetic analyses, line 92; Discussion, lines 215 and 223

### Interpretation

Among RATIFY patients aged 18-60 with FLT3-ITD AML whose 2017 ELN risk group could be assigned, the NPM1-mutated/low-allelic-ratio subgroup (>0.05 to <0.5) had favorable prognosis; 5-year overall survival was 62.5% across treatment arms and 73.0% with midostaurin.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Patients, lines 60 and 64; Results, line 176; Discussion, lines 217 and 223

### Interpretation

Among RATIFY patients aged 18-60 with FLT3-ITD AML for whom complete 2017 ELN risk categorization was possible, midostaurin had a beneficial overall-survival effect across favorable, intermediate, and adverse risk groups, with no evidence that ELN risk group modified the treatment effect; the authors also observed activity irrespective of FLT3-ITD allelic burden and NPM1 mutational status.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0010

**Category:** treatment

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

### Interpretation

Among RATIFY patients aged 18-60 with FLT3-ITD AML for whom complete 2017 ELN risk categorization was possible, adding midostaurin did not significantly increase complete-remission rates after one or two induction cycles, including across the three ELN risk groups.

## dohner-2020-npm1-flt3-interaction-C0011

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, RUNX1, ASXL1, TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Genetic analyses, line 92; Results, lines 64, 123, and 185; Discussion, lines 219 and 223

### Interpretation

Among the 318 RATIFY patients aged 18-60 with FLT3-ITD AML, the 2017 ELN adverse-risk genetic subgroup—NPM1-wild-type/high-allelic-ratio FLT3-ITD; NPM1-mutated/high-allelic-ratio FLT3-ITD with RUNX1, ASXL1, or TP53 high-risk markers; or NPM1-wild-type/low-allelic-ratio FLT3-ITD with those markers and/or adverse-risk cytogenetics—showed a strong favorable association between allogeneic HCT in first complete remission and overall survival (HR 0.39, 95% CI 0.21-0.73; P=.003). This subgroup analysis was not powered for definitive treatment comparisons.
