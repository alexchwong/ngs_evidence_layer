## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0001

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, PML, RARA, RUNX1, RUNX1T1, CBFB, MYH11, KMT2A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 3, recommendation C4; section C1 qPCR-based MRD assessment

### Interpretation

According to ELN-DAVID 2025, validated leukemia-specific MRD assays targeting NPM1, FLT3-ITD, PML::RARA, RUNX1::RUNX1T1, CBFB::MYH11, or KMT2A rearrangements are preferred over less-specific molecular markers for acute myeloid leukemia.
