## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0001

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, PML, RARA, RUNX1, RUNX1T1, CBFB, MYH11, KMT2A, WT1, MECOM

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C1. qPCR-based MRD assessment; recommendation C4

### Interpretation

In AML, validated leukemia-specific MRD assays for NPM1, FLT3-ITD, PML::RARA, RUNX1::RUNX1T1, CBFB::MYH11, and KMT2A rearrangements are preferred over less-specific WT1 or MECOM expression markers.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0002

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1, ABL1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C1. qPCR-based MRD assessment — mutNPM1 MRD

### Interpretation

For NPM1-mutated AML qPCR MRD, negativity is defined as <0.001% mutNPM1/ABL1 or meeting EAC nondetection criteria with at least 10,000 ABL1 copies; BM levels are typically about tenfold higher than PB, so tissue-specific interpretation is required.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C1. qPCR-based MRD assessment — mutNPM1 MRD after 2 cycles

### Interpretation

After two chemotherapy cycles in NPM1-mutated AML, PB—not BM—is used for the formal qPCR MRD positivity definition; a positive PB result is classified as warning rather than high risk of treatment failure because the optimal postremission treatment remains uncertain.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** A5. Clinical consequences of MRD assessment — mutNPM1 MRD relapse

### Interpretation

In NPM1-mutated AML, ELN-defined molecular MRD relapse closely precedes clinical relapse and supports considering antileukemic therapy before morphologic relapse, with alloHCT also strongly considered.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** RUNX1, RUNX1T1, CBFB, MYH11

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 5. Response criteria for MRD monitoring — CBF-AML qPCR

### Interpretation

In CBF-AML with RUNX1::RUNX1T1 or CBFB::MYH11, qPCR MRD is measured in bone marrow as CBF-mutant/ABL1 copies, with diagnostic baseline transcript quantification required. After two cycles of intensive chemotherapy or before alloHCT, a ≥3-log10 reduction from diagnostic levels is classified as an optimal response (MRD negative or low-level positive), whereas a <3-log10 reduction is MRD-positive and classified as warning.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0006

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** PML, RARA

**Diseases:** APL

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C1. qPCR-based MRD assessment — APL-MRD

### Interpretation

For PML::RARA-positive APL, routine MRD testing is recommended at end of treatment rather than during treatment; end-of-treatment positivity is a warning, and follow-up monitoring is principally reserved for high-risk patients not treated with ATO, using BM because PB may become positive only near hematologic relapse.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0007

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C2. NGS-based MRD assessment — FLT3-ITD

### Interpretation

In FLT3-ITD AML, UHS-NGS MRD is recommended after two treatment cycles and before alloHCT; EOT and follow-up use rests on more limited evidence. Positivity is above the assay LOD, which must be at least 0.01%, and NPM1 may serve as the stand-alone marker when both mutations are present and FLT3 UHS-NGS is unavailable.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0008

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** A5. Maintenance therapies; recommendation A15

### Interpretation

After alloHCT for FLT3-mutated AML, sorafenib maintenance is effective independently of pretransplant MFC-MRD status. Gilteritinib is recommended, on the basis of a MORPHO trial subgroup analysis, for patients with FLT3-ITD UHS-NGS MRD positivity before or around day 30 after alloHCT, particularly the reported subgroup with one FLT3-ITD MRD clone and mutated NPM1.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0009

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1, TP53, DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C2. NGS-based MRD assessment — recommendation C13

### Interpretation

In AML remission after intensive chemotherapy and before alloHCT, germ line mutations in predisposition-associated genes—for example ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1 and TP53—and mutations associated with age-related clonal haematopoiesis, such as DNMT3A, TET2 and ASXL1, are not prognostic and should not be used to determine MRD status at those time points.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0010

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1, ANKRD26, CEBPA, DDX41, ETV6, GATA2, RUNX1, TP53, DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C2. NGS-based MRD assessment — recommendation C14

### Interpretation

After alloHCT for AML, serial UHS-NGS monitoring of diagnostic mutations can correlate with impending relapse even for predisposition- or clonal-haematopoiesis-associated genes, but NPM1 is specifically well validated and additional genes require rigorous posttransplant validation.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0011

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** CD34, CD117, CD45, CD33, CD13, CD56, CD7, HLA-DR, CD38, CD11B, CD64, CD14, CD15, CD4, CD123, CD36

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 2, recommendation B3

### Interpretation

For AML MFC-MRD, the integrated LAIP/DfN strategy should include CD34, CD117, CD45, CD33, CD13, CD56, CD7 and HLA-DR; the recommended 10-color core and monocytic panels additionally specify CD38, CD11B, CD64, CD14, CD15, CD4, CD123 and CD36.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0012

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CD34, CD38, CD45RA, CD123, CLEC12A, CD45

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** B2. Quality requirements — leukemia stem cell MFC-MRD

### Interpretation

In AML, MFC assessment of CD34+/CD38− leukemia stem cells using aberrant CD45RA, CD123 and CLEC12A may add prognostic value beyond standard MFC-MRD; at least one million CD45-expressing cells supports higher-sensitivity measurement, but clinically actionable cutoffs still require validation.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0013

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** B2. Quality requirements — CEBPA AML

### Interpretation

In CEBPA bZIP in-frame–mutated AML, some evidence suggests that MFC-MRD is preferred after two cycles of intensive chemotherapy; its prognostic role at end of treatment and during follow-up requires further study.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0014

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** CD22, CD15

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** B1. Approach to MFC-MRD assessment — regenerative false positives

### Interpretation

In AML MFC-MRD, CD22- or CD15-associated abnormalities may reflect regenerating or stressed nonleukemic hematopoiesis rather than residual leukemia; reports should flag this possibility and consider repeat testing in 2–4 weeks when clinically indicated.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0015

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53, IDH1, CEBPA

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C2. NGS-based MRD assessment — non-FLT3 mutations

### Interpretation

For residual TP53, IDH1, or bZIP-mutated CEBPA detected by UHS-NGS in AML, prognostic effects have not been demonstrated at selected pre-alloHCT time points; gene- and timepoint-specific validation is required before these findings are used as established MRD prognostic markers.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0016

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** KMT2A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** C1. qPCR-based MRD assessment — KMT2A-rearranged AML

### Interpretation

KMT2A-rearranged AML can be assessed with developing molecular MRD assays, but persistent fusion detection at specific thresholds or time points does not yet establish a validated treatment-intervention rule.
