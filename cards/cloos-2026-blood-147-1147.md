## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0001

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** mutNPM1 MRD section, post-cycle-2 interpretation

### Interpretation

In NPM1-mutated AML after 2 chemotherapy cycles, MRD positivity is formally interpreted in peripheral blood rather than bone marrow; a positive peripheral-blood result is a warning response, not high risk of treatment failure, because the postremission transplant-versus-consolidation decision remains uncertain.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** mutNPM1 MRD section, EOT threshold paragraph

### Interpretation

For NPM1-mutated AML at end of treatment, 0.1% NPM1/ABL1 in bone marrow is the guideline MRD-positivity threshold with demonstrated prognostic significance; levels around 2% are typically close to hematologic relapse and may leave insufficient time for preemptive escalation.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0003

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 6, NPM1 qPCR peripheral-blood MRD relapse row

### Interpretation

During follow-up of NPM1-mutated AML, conversion to detectable peripheral-blood NPM1 MRD at ≥0.01% defines MRD relapse. If MRD was never negative, a ≥1-log10 rise from the nadir in the same tissue defines relapse; qPCR relapse should be confirmed within 4 weeks, preferably in paired blood and marrow.

## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0017

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 6, FLT3-ITD UHS-NGS MRD relapse row

### Interpretation

During follow-up of FLT3-ITD AML, UHS-NGS MRD at or above the assay LOD defines MRD relapse in peripheral blood or bone marrow. ## cloos-2026-blood-147-1147-C0046

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** mutNPM1 MRD section, tissue-specific thresholds

### Interpretation
