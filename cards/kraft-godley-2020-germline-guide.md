## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0008

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** BRCA1, BRCA2

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Identifying potential germline alleles from NGS assays of tumor samples, line 126

### Interpretation

A deleterious BRCA1 or BRCA2 variant detected during sequencing of a hematologic malignancy may warrant consideration of germline origin; the authors report that such variants were more commonly germline in their experience.

## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0009

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Identifying potential germline alleles from NGS assays of tumor samples, lines 57 and 126

### Interpretation

Persistence of a TP53 variant at a relatively stable VAF across serial hematopoietic samples can raise suspicion of germline origin, including in patients who do not otherwise meet clinical criteria for Li-Fraumeni syndrome; germline status requires confirmation with appropriate germline testing.
