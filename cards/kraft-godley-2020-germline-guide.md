## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Clinical case, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

In a myeloid malignancy, DDX41 p.Arg525His should not be assumed constitutional solely from its presence: the review describes it as an acquired hotspot mutation occurring in patients with deleterious germline DDX41 variants.

## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0004

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Identifying potential germline alleles from NGS assays of tumor samples, paragraph 2

### Interpretation

A truncating DDX41 variant should raise strong suspicion of germline origin because the review states that all truncating DDX41 variants reported to date were germline; suspected germline status should still be confirmed using true germline DNA.

## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0015

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Clinical case; Returning to the opening case, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

In an MDS case that progressed to AML, confirmed germline DDX41 p.Asp140fs remained detectable on serial testing but fell to VAF 0.39 on one assay, illustrating technical VAF variability; a value below 0.5 did not exclude germline origin.

## kraft-godley-2020-germline-guide-C0016

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** DDX41

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Clinical case; Returning to the opening case, Key points

### Interpretation

In the opening MDS case, DDX41 germline assessment depends on panel content because DDX41 is absent from many commercial NGS platforms; a panel lacking DDX41 cannot evaluate this gene.
