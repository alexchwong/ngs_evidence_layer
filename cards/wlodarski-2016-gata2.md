# Wlodarski MW, Hirabayashi S, Pastor V, Starý J, Hasle H, Masetti R, et al. Prevalence, clinical characteristics, and prognosis of GATA2-related myelodysplastic syndromes in children and adolescents. Blood. 2016;127(11):1387-1397.

**Publication key:** `wlodarski-2016-gata2`

## wlodarski-2016-gata2-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — prevalence of germline GATA2 mutations in pediatric primary MDS

### Interpretation

Among consecutively diagnosed pediatric primary MDS cases, germline GATA2 mutations underlay 7% overall and 15% of advanced MDS.

## wlodarski-2016-gata2-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — proposed GATA2-related MDS category

### Interpretation

The authors propose the category “MDS following GATA2 disease” or “GATA2-related MDS” for MDS arising in the setting of GATA2 disease.

## wlodarski-2016-gata2-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — GATA2 deficiency is not a poor prognostic factor for OS

### Interpretation

In consecutively diagnosed children with primary MDS, GATA2-mutated cases had inferior unadjusted overall survival, but after accounting for MDS subtype and monosomy 7 there was no overall-survival difference by GATA2 mutational status.

## wlodarski-2016-gata2-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion — HSCT outcomes by GATA2 status in pediatric MDS with monosomy 7

### Interpretation

Among pediatric patients undergoing HSCT for MDS with monosomy 7, GATA2 mutational status did not worsen transplant outcome: 5-year OS was 66% versus 69%, EFS was 60% in both groups, relapse did not significantly differ (21% versus 13%), and TRM was 21% versus 24%.

## wlodarski-2016-gata2-C0005

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — proposed GATA2 testing workup

### Interpretation

The authors propose GATA2 analysis as part of the workup for all children and young adults presenting with MDS and monosomy 7, der(1;7), or trisomy 8, irrespective of family history or accessory GATA2 disease phenotype.
