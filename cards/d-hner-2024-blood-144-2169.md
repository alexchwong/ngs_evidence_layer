## d-hner-2024-blood-144-2169-C0010

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, KRAS, NRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** HMAs plus VEN, NPM1 signaling-gene analysis

### Interpretation

In NPM1-mutated AML treated with HMA/VEN, co-occurring FLT3-ITD, KRAS, or NRAS mutations are associated with inferior prognosis; median OS was 39 months without these signaling mutations versus 9.9 months when one or more were present.

## d-hner-2024-blood-144-2169-C0011

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** IDH2, FLT3, KRAS, NRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** HMAs plus VEN, IDH2 signaling-gene analysis

### Interpretation

In IDH2-mutated AML treated with HMA/VEN, co-occurring activated signaling mutations are associated with inferior prognosis; median OS was 36.9 months without versus 12.2 months with an activated kinase pathogenic variant.

## d-hner-2024-blood-144-2169-C0012

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** IDH1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** AZA and IVO

### Interpretation

Azacitidine plus ivosidenib is a treatment standard for IDH1-mutated AML and improved overall survival versus azacitidine/placebo (24.0 vs 7.9 months) in the cited randomized phase 3 study.
