## d-hner-2024-blood-144-2169-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, FLT3, KRAS, NRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** HMAs plus VEN, paragraph 3

**Secondary citation:** Döhner H, Pratz KW, DiNardo CD, et al. Genetic risk stratification and outcomes among treatment-naive patients with AML treated with venetoclax and azacitidine. Blood. 2024;144(21):2211-2222.

### Interpretation

In NPM1-mutated AML treated with azacitidine plus venetoclax, absence of FLT3-ITD, KRAS and NRAS signaling alterations was associated with longer median overall survival than their presence (39.0 versus 9.9 months).
