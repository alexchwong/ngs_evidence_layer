## d-hner-2024-blood-144-2169-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, IDH2, FLT3, NRAS, KRAS, TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** guideline criterion

**Locator:** Table 1, risk row; Genetic risk classification applicability/limitations

### Interpretation

According to the proposed 2024 ELN Less-Intensive classification, newly diagnosed AML with mutated NPM1 or IDH2, FLT3-ITD negativity, NRAS/KRAS wild type, and TP53 wild type is favorable risk with HMA-based less-intensive therapy. This framework does not apply after prior HMA therapy and has limited relevance in patients with a prior myeloproliferative neoplasm.
