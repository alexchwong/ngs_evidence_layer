# Tefferi A, Lasho TL, Finke CM, Knudson RA, Ketterling R, Hanson CH, et al. CALR vs JAK2 vs MPL-mutated or triple-negative myelofibrosis: clinical, cytogenetic and molecular comparisons. Leukemia. 2014;28(7):1472-1477.

**Publication key:** `mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response`

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results and discussion (HMR definition and survival analysis); Introduction (MF scope)

### Interpretation

In COMFORT-II myelofibrosis (primary MF or post-PV/post-ET MF), high molecular risk (HMR), defined by at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1 or IDH2, retained adverse prognostic significance in the best-available-therapy control population; Kaplan-Meier survival at 144 weeks was 0.58 for HMR versus 0.71 for LMR.

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results and discussion (HMR definition and ruxolitinib response analysis); Introduction (MF scope)

### Interpretation

Among COMFORT-II patients with primary MF or post-PV/post-ET MF randomized to ruxolitinib, HMR status (at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1 or IDH2) did not significantly alter spleen-volume reduction or constitutional-symptom improvement at weeks 24 or 48 compared with LMR.

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results and discussion (HMR definition and ruxolitinib haematologic-toxicity analysis); Introduction (MF scope)

### Interpretation

Among COMFORT-II patients with primary MF or post-PV/post-ET MF receiving ruxolitinib, HMR status (at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1 or IDH2) did not increase the risk of ruxolitinib-associated anemia or thrombocytopenia; anemia occurred in 74% of HMR versus 72% of LMR patients, and thrombocytopenia in 34% versus 36.5%.

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Study design (survival model); Results and discussion (HMR definition and survival analysis); Introduction (MF scope)

### Interpretation

In COMFORT-II primary MF or post-PV/post-ET MF patients with HMR (at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1 or IDH2), multivariable Cox analysis adjusted for baseline IPSS category estimated a 43% lower risk of death with ruxolitinib versus best available therapy (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.30-1.08); the authors noted that the confidence intervals for the hazard ratios included 1, likely because of sample size.
