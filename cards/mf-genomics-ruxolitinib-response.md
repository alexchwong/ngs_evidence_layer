## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, SRSF2

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraph 2

**Secondary citation:** Vannucchi AM, Lasho TL, Guglielmelli P, et al. Mutations and prognosis in primary myelofibrosis. Leukemia. 2013;27(9):1861-1869.

### Interpretation

In primary myelofibrosis, high molecular risk (HMR), defined by a mutation in any of EZH2, ASXL1, IDH1, IDH2, or SRSF2, was reported as an IPSS- and DIPSS-plus-independent adverse prognostic group with shorter overall survival than low molecular risk (LMR).

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, SRSF2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results and discussion, HMR definition and survival paragraphs

### Interpretation

In the COMFORT-II molecular-analysis subset with primary or post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis, HMR (at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, or IDH2) retained adverse prognostic significance in the best-available-therapy control population; estimated survival at 144 weeks was 0.58 for HMR versus 0.71 for LMR.

## mf-genomics-ruxolitinib-response-C0008

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** ASXL1, EZH2, IDH1, IDH2, SRSF2

**Diseases:** PMF, post-PV/post-ET MF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results and discussion, HMR definition and survival paragraphs

### Interpretation

In COMFORT-II patients with primary or post-PV/post-ET myelofibrosis and HMR (at least one mutation in ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, or IDH2), multivariable Cox analysis adjusted for baseline IPSS category estimated a 43% lower risk of death with ruxolitinib versus best available therapy (HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.30-1.08); the confidence interval included 1.
