### Interpretation

In clonal haematopoiesis, U2AF1-mutant and SRSF2 p.P95H clones initiated only late in life and exhibited some of the fastest expansion dynamics; the authors relate this pattern to the high risk of progression to myeloid cancers associated with these mutations, citing prior work.
