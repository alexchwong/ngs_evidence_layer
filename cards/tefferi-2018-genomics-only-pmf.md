## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR, ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, description of MIPSS70-plus; Table 2 footnote d

**Secondary citation:** Guglielmelli P, Lasho TL, Rotunno G, Mudireddy M, Mannarelli C, Nicolosi M, et al. MIPSS70: Mutation-Enhanced International Prognostic Score System for transplantation-age patients with primary myelofibrosis. J Clin Oncol. 2018;36:310–8.

### Interpretation

For transplant-age PMF, MIPSS70-plus incorporates genetic predictors of shortened survival including absence of CALR type 1/like mutation and the presence and number of high-molecular-risk mutations, including ASXL1, SRSF2, EZH2 and IDH1/2; the model defines four risk categories with 5-year survival rates ranging from 7% to 91%.

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0012

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Development of a new risk model (GIPSS); Fig. 1

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0018

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR, ASXL1, SRSF2, U2AF1, EZH2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion, GIPSS vs MIPSS70-plus comparison

### Interpretation

For PMF patients in the intermediate GIPSS categories, the authors favor MIPSS70-plus for more comprehensive prognostication because these patients can be reclassified across MIPSS70-plus risk groups; about 20% of GIPSS intermediate-1 patients were reclassified as MIPSS70-plus high risk, and the outcome significance of this discordance remained unresolved.
