# Tefferi A, Guglielmelli P, Nicolosi M, Mannelli F, Mudireddy M, Bartalucci N, et al. GIPSS: genetically inspired prognostic scoring system for primary myelofibrosis. Leukemia. 2018;32(7):1631-1642.

**Publication key:** `tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf`

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR, ASXL1, SRSF2, U2AF1

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — overall survival multivariable analysis

### Interpretation

In 641 patients with primary myelofibrosis, absence of type 1/like CALR and the presence of ASXL1, SRSF2, or U2AF1 Q157 mutations were independent predictors of inferior overall survival in multivariable analysis restricted to genetic factors; their adverse effects remained independently significant after inclusion of DIPSS.

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** EZH2, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — overall survival analysis; Discussion

### Interpretation

In this primary myelofibrosis cohort, EZH2 and IDH1/IDH2 mutations were not statistically significant for overall survival in univariate analysis and remained non-significant in the genetic multivariable model; the authors explicitly caution that this lack of multivariable significance should not be regarded as definitive.

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — leukemia-free survival multivariable analysis

### Interpretation

In primary myelofibrosis, SRSF2 and ASXL1 mutations independently predicted inferior leukemia-free survival after multivariable adjustment that also included other risk factors for leukemic transformation, with HR 4.3 (95% CI 2.5–7.5) for SRSF2 and HR 2.1 (95% CI 1.3–3.4) for ASXL1.

## tefferi-2018-genomics-only-pmf-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR, ASXL1, SRSF2, U2AF1

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — development of GIPSS

### Interpretation

For GIPSS in primary myelofibrosis, absence of type 1/like CALR and presence of ASXL1, SRSF2, or U2AF1 Q157 each contribute one risk point. The total score defines low (0), intermediate-1 (1), intermediate-2 (2), and high (≥3) risk groups; the full category therefore depends on all GIPSS components, including karyotype.
