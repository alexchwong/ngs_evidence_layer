# Platzbecker U, Germing U, Götze KS, Kiewe P, Mayer K, Chromik J, et al. Luspatercept for the treatment of anaemia in patients with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (PACE-MDS): a multicentre, open-label phase 2 dose-finding study with long-term extension study. The Lancet Oncology. 2017;18(10):1338-1347.

**Publication key:** `sf3b1-luspatercept-response`

## sf3b1-luspatercept-response-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Methods—study design and participants; Results—higher-dose efficacy population, molecular analyses, and multivariable logistic regression

### Interpretation

In patients with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (IPSS low or intermediate 1 risk) treated with luspatercept 0·75–1·75 mg/kg in PACE-MDS, SF3B1 mutation status was associated with greater erythroid response: 24/31 (77%) SF3B1-mutated patients achieved IWG HI-E versus 6/15 (40%) SF3B1-mutation-negative patients, and SF3B1 mutation status remained significantly associated with IWG HI-E in multivariable logistic regression (yes vs no; p=0·01).
