# Mossner M, Jann J, Wittig J, Nolte F, Fey S, Nowak V, et al. Mutational hierarchies in myelodysplastic syndromes dynamically adapt and evolve upon therapy response and failure. Blood. 2016;128(9):1246-1259.

**Publication key:** `tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics`

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Figure 2 legend (P11) and Discussion paragraph beginning “Previous studies investigating del(5q) and TET2 mutations”

### Interpretation

In MDS, TET2-mutated clones can contribute to lymphoid cells; therefore, lymphocyte DNA may not be a reliable germline comparator for TET2 in some patients because MDS-associated somatic lesions can be present in lymphocytes.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0002

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** JAK2, CBL, RUNX1, ETV6

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph beginning “Our data further demonstrated that genes involved in signaling cascades”

### Interpretation

In MDS, late-acquired lesions involving JAK2 or CBL signaling genes and RUNX1 or ETV6 transcription-factor genes may serve as molecular indicators of disease progression.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0005

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** CSNK1A1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, P15 paragraph immediately before Figure 4I-J

### Interpretation

In del(5q) MDS during lenalidomide therapy, duplication of the retained chromosome 5q arm can produce copy-neutral 5q UPD that mimics cytogenetic remission; therefore, apparent cytogenetic remission does not necessarily establish elimination of the underlying molecular clone.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0006

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, paragraph beginning “To our knowledge, this study represents the first systematic comprehensive data set”

### Interpretation

Among the 14 lenalidomide-treated MDS patients in this cohort, lenalidomide reduced del(5q)-bearing clones in almost all cases but frequently did not produce complete molecular remission; marrow remained infiltrated or was replaced by clones carrying founder lesions such as TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0007

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, final paragraph beginning “In most patients, subclones appeared to originate from preexisting earlier clones”

### Interpretation

In MDS, deep and broad mutational monitoring during regular staging can help distinguish disappearance of advanced RUNX1-bearing subclones from true molecular remission and can detect advanced subclones early to anticipate disease progression and guide clinical decisions.
