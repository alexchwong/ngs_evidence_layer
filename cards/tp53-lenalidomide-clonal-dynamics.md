# Mossner M, Jann J, Wittig J, Nolte F, Fey S, Nowak V, et al. Mutational hierarchies in myelodysplastic syndromes dynamically adapt and evolve upon therapy response and failure. Blood. 2016;128(9):1246-1259.

**Publication key:** `tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics`

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0001

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** JAK2, CBL, RUNX1, ETV6

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — late molecular events as progression indicators

### Interpretation

In MDS, mutations in the signaling genes JAK2 and CBL and transcription-factor genes RUNX1 and ETV6 were almost exclusively acquired as late events, supporting their use as molecular indicators of disease progression.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0002

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Longitudinal LEN-treated cohort — persistent founder clones after treatment

### Interpretation

In the longitudinal cohort including 14 lenalidomide-treated MDS patients, lenalidomide often reduced del(5q) clones without achieving complete molecular remission; residual founder clones carrying TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A could persist, and their early detection was highlighted for clinical stratification because such clones may evolve and ultimately drive relapse.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0003

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — broad longitudinal molecular monitoring

### Interpretation

In MDS, deep and broad mutational monitoring during regular staging can help distinguish disappearance of advanced RUNX1-bearing subclones from true molecular remission and can detect advanced subclones early to anticipate disease progression and guide clinical decisions.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2, DNMT3A, ATM

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — P45 before and after 5 months of lenalidomide

### Interpretation

In MDS patient P45, after 5 months of lenalidomide the DNMT3A- and ATM-containing later molecular lesions were substantially reduced, whereas the candidate TET2 founder mutation persisted almost completely; this is single-patient longitudinal treatment-response evidence.

## tp53-lenalidomide-clonal-dynamics-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** SF3B1, TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — P13 lenalidomide response loss and clonal progression

### Interpretation

In MDS patient P13 receiving lenalidomide, loss of drug response coincided with reappearance of the initial del(5q) subclone carrying newly acquired SF3B1 and TP53 mutations; this clone subsequently dominated through progression to secondary AML. This is a single-patient temporal association and does not establish TP53 or SF3B1 as general predictors of lenalidomide resistance.
