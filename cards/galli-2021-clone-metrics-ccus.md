# Gallì A, Todisco G, Catamo E, Sala C, Elena C, Pozzi S, et al. Relationship between clone metrics and clinical outcome in clonal cytopenia. Blood. 2021;138(11):965-976.

**Publication key:** `galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus`

## galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, TET2, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Clonal features and risk of progression to an MN in patients with cytopenia of undetermined significance

### Interpretation

In the prospective ICUS cohort, somatic genetic lesions defined CCUS; in competing-risk analyses of progression to myeloid neoplasm, ASXL1 and TET2 mutations were associated with significantly increased risk (HR 4.2 and 3.7, respectively; P<.005), whereas DNMT3A mutation was not associated with a significant increase in risk.

## galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, SF3B1, TP53

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Unsupervised clustering analysis throughout the spectrum of clinical phenotypes

### Interpretation

Unsupervised mutation-profile clustering across CHIP, CH-AoE, CCUS, and myeloid neoplasm without excess blasts identified a CH-like cluster mainly driven by isolated DNMT3A and an MN-like cluster characterized by more complex profiles, including TP53 or DTA comutations and isolated TET2, ASXL1, or SF3B1. Among patients with CCUS, the CH-like and MN-like clusters had significantly different cumulative incidence of progression to myeloid neoplasm in both univariable and multivariable competing-risk analyses (HR 2.73; P<.001).

## galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, U2AF1, TP53

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Clone metrics segregate distinct subsets of clonal cytopenia; high-clinical-expressivity subset

### Interpretation

Among patients with CCUS in the MN-like cluster, a high-clinical-expressivity subset (22%) included patterns involving SF3B1, U2AF1, or TP53, as well as DTA genes with additional mutations. These clones had VAFs below overt-dysplasia thresholds and lower than myeloid neoplasms with similar profiles; in multivariable analyses, this subset had a significantly higher cumulative incidence of progression to myeloid neoplasm than individuals without mutation or with a CH-like signature (HR 5.02; P<.001).

## galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Clone metrics segregate distinct subsets of clonal cytopenia; low-clinical-expressivity subset

### Interpretation

In CCUS, the MN-like cluster was defined by unsupervised mutation profiling and mainly comprised splicing-factor, TP53, or DTA-plus-other mutation patterns (DTA = DNMT3A, TET2, and ASXL1). Within this cluster, a subset comprising 45% of patients—mainly involving SRSF2 mutations, combinations of DTA mutations, or multiple mutations outside the DTA, splicing-factor, and TP53 groups—had a significantly higher cumulative incidence of progression to a myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia (MN) than individuals without mutations or with a CH-like signature (adjusted HR 11.28; P<.001).

## galli-2021-clone-metrics-ccus-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Clone metrics segregate distinct subsets of clonal cytopenia; isolated TET2/ASXL1 subset

### Interpretation

Among patients with CCUS in the MN-like cluster, a third subset (25%) included isolated TET2 or ASXL1 mutations or isolated mutations in genes outside the splicing-factor/DTA/TP53 groups. Its VAFs were similar to the CH-like cluster and lower than in myeloid neoplasms with the same drivers; overall survival did not differ significantly from the CH-like cluster (P=.56), while cumulative incidence of progression to myeloid neoplasm was only marginally higher (P=.06).
