# Davidsson J, Puschmann A, Tedgård U, Bryder D, Nilsson L, Cammenga J. SAMD9 and SAMD9L in inherited predisposition to ataxia, pancytopenia, and myeloid malignancies. Leukemia. 2018;32(5):1106-1115.

**Publication key:** `davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l`

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

Germline gain-of-function mutations in SAMD9 or SAMD9L are associated with the MIRAGE, ataxia–pancytopenia, and myelodysplasia/leukemia-with-monosomy-7 syndromic spectrum; they increase the normal antiproliferative effect of SAMD9/SAMD9L and can cause pancytopenia with restricted growth or organ hypoplasia.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2, SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Diagnosis and follow-up > Screening for SAMD9 and SAMD9L mutations

### Interpretation

All pediatric MDS patients with monosomy 7, del(7q), or der(1;7), and probably adult MDS patients with the same chromosome 7 abnormalities, should undergo gene-panel testing covering GATA2-associated disorders and including SAMD9 and SAMD9L.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0003

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Diagnosis and follow-up > Screening for SAMD9 and SAMD9L mutations

### Interpretation

For SAMD9/SAMD9L testing in chromosome-7-abnormal MDS, the authors recommend genetic material not obtained from blood; they consider skin fibroblasts the best germline-DNA source because buccal swabs can show non-representative skewing toward wild type, likely from contamination by blood cells that underwent CN-LOH reversion.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0004

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Diagnosis and follow-up > Screening for SAMD9 and SAMD9L mutations

### Interpretation

When MIRAGE or ATXPC syndrome is diagnosed in one family member, relatives should, after consent, be offered genetic counseling and testing for the identified familial mutation; counseling should address possible MDS or cytopenia risk. In ATXPC families, testing may additionally be relevant when considering relatives as potential HSC donors.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SAMD9, SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Abstract

### Interpretation

In carriers of germline SAMD9/SAMD9L gain-of-function mutations, somatic chromosome 7 loss or secondary loss-of-function mutations can clonally reverse the germline antiproliferative effect; complete or partial loss of 7q may cause MDS.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Treatment of MDS in SAMD9L and SAMD9 mutation carriers

### Interpretation

For SAMD9L-associated ATXPC patients who develop MDS, the review states that evidence is insufficient to define optimal HSCT use; it warns of possible neurological harm and advises careful risk-benefit assessment, noting that myeloablative conditioning possibly triggered cerebellar atrophy in one patient.

## davidsson-2018-samd9-samd9l-C0008

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** SAMD9L

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Treatment of MDS in SAMD9L and SAMD9 mutation carriers

### Interpretation

In the MDS-treatment context for SAMD9L-associated ATXPC, the authors advise avoiding higher doses of irradiation, especially to the brain; they explicitly state that whether irradiation causes the same cellular damage suspected from conditioning is unknown.
