**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Main text, Fig. 3a–b; line 466

## abelson-2018-predict-aml-C0008

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Fig. 3a legend, lines 462–464; Methods, Curation of oncogenic variants, line 1044

### Interpretation

RUNX1 variants detected by sequencing without a matched normal/germline control may not be readily distinguishable as somatic versus germline. Because deleterious germline RUNX1 variants are relatively prevalent, the RUNX1 hazard ratio for AML risk reported in this study should therefore be interpreted with caution.
