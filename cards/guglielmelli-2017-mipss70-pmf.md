# Guglielmelli P, Lasho TL, Rotunno G, Mudireddy M, Mannarelli C, Nicolosi M, et al. MIPSS70: Mutation-Enhanced International Prognostic Score System for Transplantation-Age Patients With Primary Myelofibrosis. Journal of Clinical Oncology. 2018;36(4):310-318.

**Publication key:** `guglielmelli-2017-mipss70-pmf`

## guglielmelli-2017-mipss70-pmf-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** CALR

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Development and Validation of Mutation-Enhanced IPSS: MIPSS70; Development and Validation of the Cytogenetic-Enhanced MIPSS70 Scoring System: MIPSS70-Plus; Table 2

### Interpretation

According to the MIPSS70 and MIPSS70-plus frameworks, absence of a type 1-like CALR mutation in patients aged 70 years or younger with primary myelofibrosis is associated with inferior overall survival.

## guglielmelli-2017-mipss70-pmf-C0003

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Development and Validation of Mutation-Enhanced IPSS: MIPSS70; Development and Validation of the Cytogenetic-Enhanced MIPSS70 Scoring System: MIPSS70-Plus; Table 2

According to the MIPSS70 and MIPSS70-plus frameworks, the presence of mutations in two or more of ASXL1, EZH2, SRSF2, IDH1, and IDH2 in patients aged 70 years or younger with primary myelofibrosis identifies a particularly adverse overall-survival group.

## guglielmelli-2017-mipss70-pmf-C0004

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Development and Validation of the Cytogenetic-Enhanced MIPSS70 Scoring System: MIPSS70-Plus; Table 3; Figure 1C-D
