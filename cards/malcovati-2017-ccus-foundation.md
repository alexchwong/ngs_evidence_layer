**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, SRSF2, SF3B1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, JAK2, TP53, U2AF1, EZH2, IDH2, ZRSR2, NRAS, IDH1, STAG2, BCOR, CBL, CUX1, KRAS, GATA2, PTPN11, CALR, ETV6, PHF6, KIT, NPM1, MLL, GNAS, ATRX, CDKN2A, KDM6A, MPL, WT1, ABL1, RAD21, FLT3, CEBPA, GATA1

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Predictive value of mutation status and hematopoietic clone size

### Interpretation

In patients investigated for unexplained cytopenia, a negative result across the studied 40-gene panel did not exclude the study's composite myeloid-neoplasm endpoint (MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, or AML): the negative predictive value was 0.76, increasing to 0.84 when mutation status was combined with standard karyotyping.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, SRSF2, SF3B1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, JAK2, TP53, U2AF1, EZH2, IDH2, ZRSR2, NRAS, IDH1, STAG2, BCOR, CBL, CUX1, KRAS, GATA2, PTPN11, CALR, ETV6, PHF6, KIT, NPM1, MLL, GNAS, ATRX, CDKN2A, KDM6A, MPL, WT1, ABL1, RAD21, FLT3, CEBPA, GATA1

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Predictive value of mutation status and hematopoietic clone size

### Interpretation

In patients investigated for unexplained cytopenia, finding two or more somatic mutations in the studied 40-gene panel was strongly predictive of the study's composite myeloid-neoplasm endpoint (MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, or AML), with positive predictive value 0.88 (95% CI 0.84-0.92). These predictive findings were reported as fully confirmed in the independent 190-patient validation cohort.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion — clone size and VAF

### Interpretation

For mutations measured in isolated peripheral-blood granulocytes, a VAF around 0.10 was associated with the best classification of the study's composite myeloid-neoplasm endpoint (MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, or AML); the paper cautions that the applicable allele-burden threshold may vary when a different blood-cell source is used. These predictive findings were reported as fully confirmed in the independent 190-patient validation cohort.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0004

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2, RUNX1

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, mutations in SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2, or RUNX1 had the highest predictive values for the study's composite myeloid-neoplasm endpoint (MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, or AML), with positive predictive values of 0.88-0.97 irrespective of co-occurring mutations. These predictive findings were reported as fully confirmed in the independent 190-patient validation cohort.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0005

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TET2, DNMT3A, ASXL1

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, isolated TET2, DNMT3A, or ASXL1 mutations were substantially less predictive of the study's composite myeloid-neoplasm endpoint (MDS, MDS/MPN, MPN, or AML) than the same mutations occurring with additional genetic lesions (positive predictive value 0.29-0.57 vs 0.81-0.89). These predictive findings were reported as fully confirmed in the independent 190-patient validation cohort.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, SRSF2, SF3B1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, JAK2, TP53, U2AF1, EZH2, IDH2, ZRSR2, NRAS, IDH1, STAG2, BCOR, CBL, CUX1, KRAS, GATA2, PTPN11, CALR, ETV6, PHF6, KIT, NPM1, MLL, GNAS, ATRX, CDKN2A, KDM6A, MPL, WT1, ABL1, RAD21, FLT3, CEBPA, GATA1

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Diagnostic value of mutation status in patients with cytopenia of undetermined significance

### Interpretation

Among patients provisionally diagnosed with ICUS, mutation-positive CCUS carried a markedly higher risk of progression to myeloid neoplasm than absence of clonality (HR 13.9; 95% CI 5.40-35.91); 5- and 10-year progression probabilities were 82% and 95% versus 9% and 9%, respectively.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0007

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — CCUS progression by mutation pattern; Discussion — CCUS progression by mutation pattern

### Interpretation

In CCUS, the study-defined high-predictive mutation-pattern group comprised either (1) an SF3B1, SRSF2, or U2AF1 mutation, whether isolated or co-mutated, or (2) a TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A mutation with ≥1 additional mutation. This pooled group had a 95% 5-year cumulative probability of developing a myeloid neoplasm.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0009

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms

### Interpretation

For patients diagnosed with MDS on the basis of mild dysplasia (<25% dysplastic cells), absence of detectable clonality by standard cytogenetics and targeted sequencing, together with a stable course, raised concern that the MDS diagnosis might be inappropriate despite meeting then-current WHO criteria.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0010

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2

**Diseases:** PMF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms

### Interpretation

Primary myelofibrosis with JAK2 co-mutation was the stated specificity exception for SRSF2 or U2AF1: after excluding this pattern, SRSF2 and U2AF1 mutations showed 100% specificity for myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, ZRSR2, TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, IDH1, IDH2, JAK2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion — highly specific mutation patterns and MDS classification

### Interpretation

In cases with inconclusive dysplasia, highly specific mutation patterns may provide presumptive evidence of MDS even without definitive morphology. The reported patterns included splicing-factor mutations and co-mutation patterns involving TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A with RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, or IDH1/IDH2; SRSF2/U2AF1 with JAK2 in primary myelofibrosis was a stated specificity exception.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0013

**Category:** diagnosis

**Secondary citation:** Arber DA, Orazi A, Hasserjian R, et al. The 2016 revision to the World Health Organization classification of myeloid neoplasms and acute leukemia. Blood. 2016;127(20):2391-2405.
