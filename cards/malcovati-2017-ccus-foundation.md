# Malcovati L, Gallì A, Travaglino E, Ambaglio I, Rizzo E, Molteni E, et al. Clinical significance of somatic mutation in unexplained blood cytopenia. Blood. 2017;129(25):3371-3378.

**Publication key:** `malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation`

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results—Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, SF3B1 mutation strongly supports an underlying myeloid neoplasm, unspecified by subtype, including when SF3B1 is the only detected mutation.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, ZRSR2, JAK2, RUNX1

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns; Discussion

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, SRSF2, U2AF1, ZRSR2, JAK2, or RUNX1 mutation strongly supports an underlying myeloid neoplasm, unspecified by subtype, whether detected alone or with other mutations.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0004

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TET2, DNMT3A, ASXL1

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns; Discussion

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, an isolated TET2, DNMT3A, or ASXL1 mutation provides substantially less support for an underlying myeloid neoplasm, unspecified by subtype, than the same mutation occurring with additional genetic lesions.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0005

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53, TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, TP53 mutation is more supportive of an underlying myeloid neoplasm, unspecified by subtype, when co-mutated—most often with TET2 or ASXL1—than when TP53 is isolated.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0006

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TET2, DNMT3A, ASXL1, RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, TP53, NRAS, CUX1, IDH2

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, co-mutation patterns in which TET2, DNMT3A, or ASXL1 co-occurs with RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, TP53, NRAS, CUX1, or IDH2 are highly predictive of an underlying myeloid neoplasm, unspecified by subtype.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0007

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, SF3B1 mutation is highly specific for a myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia—MDS, MDS/MPN, or AML with myelodysplasia-related changes—even when SF3B1 is the only detected mutation.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0010

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2

**Diseases:** MDS, MDS/MPN, AML, PMF

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms

### Interpretation

In unexplained cytopenia, SRSF2 or U2AF1 mutation is highly specific for a myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia—MDS, MDS/MPN, or AML with myelodysplasia-related changes—except when co-occurring with JAK2 in primary myelofibrosis.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0011

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, ZRSR2, TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** CCUS, MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms; Discussion

### Interpretation

In patients with CCUS and inconclusive dysplasia, the authors propose that highly specific mutation patterns—SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, or ZRSR2 mutation, or TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A co-mutated with RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, IDH1, or IDH2—may provide presumptive evidence of MDS even when definitive morphologic criteria are absent.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0013

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, ZRSR2, TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Diagnostic value of mutation status in patients with cytopenia of undetermined significance; Discussion

### Interpretation

In CCUS, spliceosome mutations in SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, or ZRSR2, or TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A mutations with additional mutations, identify a very high risk of progression to a myeloid neoplasm.
