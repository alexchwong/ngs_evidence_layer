# Malcovati L, Gallì A, Travaglino E, Ambaglio I, Rizzo E, Molteni E, et al. Clinical significance of somatic mutation in unexplained blood cytopenia. Blood. 2017;129(25):3371-3378.

**Publication key:** `malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation`

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Positive predictive value for myeloid neoplasm of mutation patterns in the differential diagnosis of unexplained cytopenia; multivariable logistic regression

### Interpretation

In the unexplained-cytopenia cohort, SF3B1 mutation was an independent predictor of the composite diagnosis of MDS or another myeloid neoplasm in multivariable logistic regression (OR 4.83; P = .003).

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SF3B1

**Diseases:** CCUS, MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms; SF3B1 specificity and CCUS cases

### Interpretation

In an analysis designed to assess presumptive molecular evidence of MDS when definitive morphology was absent, SF3B1 mutation had 0.97 specificity for myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia whether isolated or co-mutated. Three initially CCUS cases with SF3B1 VAF 0.05-0.11 and 1%-3% ring sideroblasts later developed overt MDS; the study concluded that highly specific mutation patterns may provide presumptive evidence of MDS when morphology is inconclusive.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, U2AF1, JAK2

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms; SRSF2/U2AF1 specificity with JAK2-associated primary myelofibrosis exclusion

### Interpretation

In an analysis designed to assess presumptive molecular evidence of MDS when definitive morphology was absent, SRSF2 and U2AF1 mutations were 100% specific for myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia only after excluding the specific JAK2-associated primary myelofibrosis co-mutation pattern; highly specific patterns were proposed as presumptive evidence of MDS when definitive morphology was absent.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0004

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** ZRSR2, TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A, RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** CCUS, MDS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Implications of mutation status for the diagnosis of myeloid neoplasms; additional highly specific mutation patterns

### Interpretation

In an analysis designed to assess presumptive molecular evidence of MDS when definitive morphology was absent, ZRSR2 mutation was among additional findings with high specificity for myeloid neoplasm with myelodysplasia. Co-mutation patterns involving TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A with RUNX1, EZH2, CBL, BCOR, CUX1, TP53, or IDH1/IDH2 as frequent co-occurring mutations had specificity >0.90; in CCUS, such highly specific patterns were proposed as presumptive evidence of MDS.

## malcovati-2017-ccus-foundation-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2, ASXL1, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Diagnostic value of mutation status in patients with cytopenia of undetermined significance; Discussion — CCUS progression risk

### Interpretation

Among patients provisionally diagnosed with ICUS, mutations in TET2, ASXL1, or DNMT3A with additional mutations were included among patterns classified as highly predictive for myeloid neoplasm. Across the pooled high-predictive pattern group, which also included spliceosome gene mutations, progression risk was higher than for low-predictive patterns (HR 4.12; 95% CI 1.75-9.69) or unmutated status (HR 25.26; 95% CI 9.27-68.86), and the group had a 5-year cumulative probability of developing a myeloid neoplasm of 95%.
