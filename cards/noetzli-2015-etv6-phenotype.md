# Noetzli L, Lo RW, Lee-Sherick AB, Callaghan M, Noris P, Savoia A, et al. Germline mutations in ETV6 are associated with thrombocytopenia, red cell macrocytosis and predisposition to lymphoblastic leukemia. Nat Genet. 2015 May;47(5):535-538.

**Publication key:** `noetzli-2015-etv6-phenotype`

## noetzli-2015-etv6-phenotype-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** ETV6

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract and summary paragraph

### Interpretation

Germline ETV6 c.641C>T (p.Pro214Leu) was identified in two families with thrombocytopenia and elevated red-cell MCV; leukemia predisposition was observed in both p.Pro214Leu families.

## noetzli-2015-etv6-phenotype-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** ETV6

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Family 3 mutation paragraph and c.1252A>G splice-analysis paragraph

### Interpretation

Germline ETV6 c.1252A>G (p.Arg418Gly) was identified in family 3, whose affected individuals had platelet counts of 99,000–101,000/µl and red-cell MCV of 93–98 fL but no malignancies; RNA from individuals carrying the variant showed exon-7 skipping.
