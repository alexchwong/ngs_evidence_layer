# Cuellar-Rodriguez J, Gea-Banacloche J, Freeman AF, Hsu AP, Zerbe CS, Calvo KR, et al. Successful allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation for GATA2 deficiency. Blood. 2011;118(13):3715-3720.

**Publication key:** `hsu-2011-gata2-momomac`

## hsu-2011-gata2-momomac-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results and discussion, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

In familial MonoMAC, heterozygous GATA2 mutations can be germline: in kindreds 1 and 13, the same mutation was identified in the proband and an affected relative, confirming germline transmission.

## hsu-2011-gata2-momomac-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraph 2

### Interpretation

GATA2 mutations were reported to cause autosomal dominant familial MDS/AML; in the cited four families, disease began from the teens to 40s without preceding problems before rapid MDS or AML onset.

### Interpretation
