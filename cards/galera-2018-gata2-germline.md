# Galera P, Hsu AP, Wang W, Droll S, Chen R, Schwartz JR, et al. Donor-derived MDS/AML in families with germline GATA2 mutation. Blood. 2018;132(18):1994-1998.

**Publication key:** `galera-2018-gata2-germline`

## galera-2018-gata2-germline-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Opening background paragraph (paper.md line 9)

### Interpretation

Germline heterozygous GATA2 mutations causing GATA2 deficiency are associated with a high propensity for transformation to MDS/AML; the paper cites an estimated lifetime MDS/AML risk of 75% to 90%, with a median age of onset of 20 years, while noting highly variable age of presentation, phenotype, and progression.

## galera-2018-gata2-germline-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Three-family related-donor outcome summary (paper.md line 11)

### Interpretation

For HSCT in families with germline GATA2 mutations, mutation-positive related donors should be avoided even when clinically healthy, because the three reported families included donor-derived posttransplant MDS/AML, recurrent MDS/AML, or fatal infection due to impaired immune reconstitution after transplantation from a mutation-positive healthy related donor.

## galera-2018-gata2-germline-C0003

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** GATA2

**Diseases:** MDS/AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Concluding germline-screening and related-donor recommendations (paper.md line 123)

### Interpretation

The authors recommend germline GATA2 screening for all pediatric, adolescent, and young adult patients diagnosed with MDS/AML, regardless of family history or phenotypic manifestations, particularly when a related donor is being considered for HSCT; healthy potential related donors should be tested for the patient's mutation before HSCT to avoid donor-derived MDS/AML, failed engraftment, or failed immune reconstitution.
