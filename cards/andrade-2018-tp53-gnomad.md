 variants: A gnomAD-based analysis. Human Mutation. 2018;40(1):97-105.

**Publication key:** `andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad`

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0001

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Section 1 INTRODUCTION, opening paragraph

### Interpretation

Germline TP53 variants are the primary genetic cause of Li–Fraumeni syndrome (LFS), a hereditary cancer-predisposition disorder associated with high lifetime cancer risk; the paper states that LFS diagnosis is based on clinical criteria incorporating personal and family history of cancer.

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0002

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Section 4 DISCUSSION, paragraph on gnomAD quality control and somatic TP53 calls

### Interpretation

Somatic TP53 variants can complicate genetic testing for cancer predisposition genes and may be misinterpreted as germline; in this study context, gnomAD used an alternate-allele-fraction threshold greater than 0.2 to reduce somatic calls, but the authors state that this may not fully exclude false-positive variants arising from clonal hematopoiesis.

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0003

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Section 4 DISCUSSION, paragraph on p.N235S, p.R290H, and p.V31I

### Interpretation

For germline TP53 p.N235S, the paper reports discordant pathogenicity evidence: HGMD classifies it as disease causing, but it has tolerant in silico scores, lacks evidence of segregation with disease and functional consequences, has a Finnish gnomAD MAF of 0.054%, and is described as likely benign or VUS by most clinical pipelines.

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0004

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Section 4 DISCUSSION, paragraph on p.N235S, p.R290H, and p.V31I

### Interpretation

For germline TP53 p.R290H, the paper reports that it is often considered a VUS in clinical practice because of limited LFS phenotypic evidence, supertrans yeast activity, apparently normal in vitro functional activity, and possible lack of segregation; its occurrence in some LFS families and likely-pathogenic classification by some laboratories warrant further evaluation of its true pathogenic potential.

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0005

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Section 4 DISCUSSION, paragraph on p.N235S, p.R290H, and p.V31I

### Interpretation

For germline TP53 p.V31I, the paper reports exclusive identification in individuals of East-Asian ancestry in gnomAD at 0.3% and states that its high representation may indicate a moderate-risk allele or a population-specific variant in individuals of East-Asian ancestry.

## andrade-2018-tp53-gnomad-C0006

**Category:** germline

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** —

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Section 4 DISCUSSION, concluding paragraph

### Interpretation

The authors caution that pathogenic or likely pathogenic germline TP53 variants found in atypical clinical settings should be comprehensively evaluated based on individual cancer risk, and that LFS management strategies should be indicated only after thorough discussion with specialists; this recommendation is framed in the context of reduced penetrance, variable phenotype, and discordant variant classification.
