## tefferi-2020-mipss-et-pv-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1, TP53

**Diseases:** ET

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — merged Mayo/Florence MIPSS analysis

### Interpretation

In the merged Mayo/Florence ET analysis, presence of any adverse mutation (SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1 or TP53) independently predicted worse survival (HR 2.4, 95% CI 1.6–3.5) and contributed two adverse points to the combined-analysis MIPSS-ET model.

## tefferi-2020-mipss-et-pv-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** SRSF2

**Diseases:** PV

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — merged Mayo/Florence MIPSS analysis

### Interpretation

In the merged Mayo/Florence PV analysis, SRSF2 was the adverse mutation; its presence independently predicted worse survival (HR 7.8, 95% CI 3.1–17.0) and contributed three adverse points to the combined-analysis MIPSS-PV model.

## tefferi-2020-mipss-et-pv-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** ET, PV

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results — Mutations and prognosis, JAK2 VAF paragraph

### Interpretation

Across ET and PV, JAK2 mutation VAF did not show an independent effect on overall, myelofibrosis-free, or leukaemia-free survival; the underlying VAF analysis was reported as data not shown.
