# DiNardo CD, Tiong IS, Quaglieri A, MacRaild S, Loghavi S, Brown FC, et al. Molecular patterns of response and treatment failure after frontline venetoclax combinations in older patients with AML. Blood. 2020;135(11):791-803.

**Publication key:** `dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax`

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1, IDH1, IDH2, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular characteristics of patients with durable remission

### Interpretation

In this cohort of older patients with AML receiving frontline venetoclax-based combinations, NPM1-, IDH1-, IDH2-, or DNMT3A-mutated cases had CR/CRi rates exceeding 80%, compared with 64% in the overall population.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1, IDH2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular characteristics of patients with durable remission

### Interpretation

Among older AML patients treated with venetoclax plus DNMTi or LDAC, NPM1 and/or IDH2 mutation was associated with high treatment response, durable remission, and prolonged survival; 2-year OS was 71.8% for NPM1-mutated and 79.5% for IDH2-mutated AML, with median OS not reached.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0003

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular characteristics of patients with durable remission; Discussion — summary

### Interpretation

In NPM1-mutated AML treated with venetoclax-based combinations, NPM1 was suppressed to undetectable levels by highly sensitive RT-qPCR in all 4 assessed remission cases for at least 24 months; the authors proposed molecular MRD monitoring for the favorable-risk NPM1-mutated subgroup in future clinical trials.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** IDH1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular characteristics of patients with durable remission

### Interpretation

In this venetoclax-treated AML cohort, IDH1 mutation was not associated with a statistically significant overall-survival difference versus IDH1 wild-type AML (median 18.3 vs 12.7 months; P=.79), and its prognostic association was less clear than for IDH2.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Adaptive resistance to venetoclax combinations is associated with kinase activation

### Interpretation

FLT3-ITD did not preclude an initial response to frontline venetoclax combinations in older AML, but adaptive resistance was associated with FLT3-ITD expansion or acquisition at relapse; this occurred in 5 serially studied cases within 1–6 months of initial remission.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0006

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Clones with biallelic TP53 defects enriched at relapse under selective venetoclax-chemotherapy pressure

### Interpretation

In relapsing AML after venetoclax-based combination therapy, TP53-mutated clones expanded in all 5 serially sampled cases, and 3 acquired accompanying 17p loss, supporting evolution of likely biallelic TP53 perturbation as an adaptive resistance pattern.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — TP53 loss promotes resistance to both venetoclax and chemotherapy

### Interpretation

Functional AML models supported TP53 dysfunction or loss as a cause of reduced effectiveness of venetoclax-based combinations; in a p53 R172H model, resistance to venetoclax plus azacitidine or cytarabine was striking despite only a marginal effect on venetoclax alone.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0008

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — TP53 perturbation and venetoclax resistance

### Interpretation

For AML receiving venetoclax-based therapy, the authors recommend surveying TP53 genetic integrity at diagnosis and during remission to identify patients likely to develop resistance; monoallelic TP53 abnormality did not necessarily preclude an initial remission, whereas biallelic defects were common in refractory disease or evolved at relapse.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular patterns of primary resistance to venetoclax chemotherapy

### Interpretation

RUNX1 mutation was present in 40% of primary refractory AML cases and was expanded or acquired at relapse in 3 patients, but the study explicitly considered its role as a driver of venetoclax resistance uncertain because RUNX1-mutated cases also occurred among patients with long remissions.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0010

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NRAS, KRAS, CBL, KIT

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular patterns of primary resistance to venetoclax chemotherapy

### Interpretation

Activating signaling mutations were enriched in primary refractory AML treated with venetoclax combinations: FLT3-ITD, N/KRAS, CBL, or KIT occurred in each of the 4 refractory cases outside the TP53/RUNX1 groups, and signaling-pathway mutations were present in 60% of refractory versus 27% of durable-remission cases.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0011

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — durable remission; Discussion — favorable long-term prognosis

### Interpretation

IDH1- or IDH2-mutated AML showed high response rates to venetoclax combinations, but IDH-mutant clones commonly persisted; the authors therefore suggested that post-remission IDH-inhibitor maintenance may be required to extinguish residual IDH-mutant clones, with particularly favorable survival observed for IDH2-mutated AML.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0012

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — favorable long-term prognosis with venetoclax-based therapy

### Interpretation

For elderly NPM1-mutated AML, this study observed durable remission, sustained RT-qPCR MRD negativity, and 2-year OS exceeding 70% with venetoclax plus DNMTi or LDAC; the authors therefore suggested venetoclax may be the preferred therapeutic option for this molecular subgroup.
