## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0016

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Adaptive resistance to venetoclax combinations is associated with kinase activation

### Interpretation

In AML treated with venetoclax combinations, adaptive resistance was associated with FLT3-ITD clonal expansion or acquisition at relapse; this occurred within 1-6 months of initial remission in five cases, despite initial responses being comparable to the overall cohort.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0018

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — TP53 resistance; Results — TP53 loss promotes resistance

### Interpretation

Biallelic TP53 disruption was strongly associated with primary or adaptive resistance to venetoclax combined with a DNMT inhibitor or cytarabine, whereas a monoallelic TP53 mutation or del(17p) did not by itself preclude an initial remission.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0019

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** RUNX1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular patterns of primary resistance to venetoclax chemotherapy

### Interpretation

RUNX1 mutations were frequent in primary refractory AML, but RUNX1-mutated cases also occurred among long remissions; the study therefore did not establish RUNX1 mutation as a driver of venetoclax resistance.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0020

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Molecular patterns of primary resistance to venetoclax chemotherapy

### Interpretation

In one morphologically refractory NPM1-mutated AML case treated with venetoclax-azacitidine, NPM1 RT-qPCR fell from 100% to 7.3% during cycle 1 despite no blast response, demonstrating that molecular and morphologic response can be discordant.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0022

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — favorable long-term prognosis with venetoclax-based therapy

### Interpretation

For elderly NPM1-mutated AML, the study's >70% 2-year overall survival with venetoclax plus a DNMT inhibitor or LDAC led the authors to suggest venetoclax as a preferred therapeutic option for this subgroup.
