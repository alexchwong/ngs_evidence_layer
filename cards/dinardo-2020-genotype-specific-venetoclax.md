# DiNardo CD, Tiong IS, Quaglieri A, MacRaild S, Loghavi S, Brown FC, et al. Molecular patterns of response and treatment failure after frontline venetoclax combinations in older patients with AML. Blood. 2020;135(11):791-803.

**Publication key:** `dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax`

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraphs 1-2

### Interpretation

For older or unfit patients with AML, venetoclax combined with a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor or low-dose cytarabine is an approved lower-intensity treatment option.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NPM1, IDH1, IDH2, DNMT3A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Molecular characteristics of patients with durable remission, paragraph 1

### Interpretation

In older patients with AML treated with frontline venetoclax plus a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor or low-dose cytarabine, NPM1, IDH1, IDH2, or DNMT3A mutation is associated with a high likelihood of initial CR/CRi response.

In older patients with AML receiving frontline venetoclax-based combinations, NPM1 or IDH2 mutation is associated with durable remission.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract and Key Points—adaptive resistance

In AML treated with venetoclax-based combinations, adaptive resistance is associated with expansion or acquisition of activated kinase-signaling clones, particularly FLT3-ITD.

**Locator:** Results—Clones with biallelic TP53 defects enriched at relapse under selective venetoclax-chemotherapy pressure

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph 3

### Interpretation

In AML treated with venetoclax combined with a DNA methyltransferase inhibitor or cytarabine, a monoallelic TP53 mutation or del(17p) does not by itself preclude an initial remission.

In AML treated with venetoclax-based combinations, biallelic TP53 abnormalities present at diagnosis are associated with primary refractory disease.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0011

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph 3

For AML treated with venetoclax-based therapy, the study indicates the importance of assessing TP53 genetic integrity at diagnosis and during remission to identify patients likely to develop treatment resistance.

## dinardo-2020-genotype-specific-venetoclax-C0013

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NRAS, KRAS, CBL, KIT

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results—Molecular patterns of primary resistance to venetoclax chemotherapy, paragraph 2

### Interpretation

In primary refractory AML after venetoclax-based combinations, activating signaling-pathway mutations are a recurrent resistance pattern; FLT3, NRAS/KRAS, CBL, and KIT alterations were observed in primary refractory cases outside the TP53- and RUNX1-mutated groups.
