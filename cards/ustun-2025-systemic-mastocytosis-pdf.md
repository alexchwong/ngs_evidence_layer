Detection of KIT D816V or another activating KIT mutation in bone marrow, peripheral blood, or another extracutaneous organ is a minor diagnostic criterion for systemic mastocytosis.

## ustun-2025-systemic-mastocytosis-pdf-C0018

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** What is next after making a diagnosis of SM?

### Interpretation

For systemic mastocytosis, KIT D816V variant allele fraction of at least 10% in bone marrow or peripheral-blood leukocytes is a WHO B-finding used in smoldering systemic mastocytosis classification; ICC does not use this molecular B-finding.

**Locator:** How to diagnose SM?; bone-marrow immunophenotyping paragraph

### Interpretation

In systemic mastocytosis evaluation, CD117/KIT and mast-cell tryptase identify mast cells but cannot establish a neoplastic mast-cell phenotype because they do not distinguish abnormal from normal mast cells.

## ustun-2025-systemic-mastocytosis-pdf-C0042

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** How to diagnose SM?; bone-marrow immunophenotyping paragraph

### Interpretation

In systemic mastocytosis evaluation, mast-cell tryptase and CD117/KIT should both be assessed because neoplastic mast cells can degranulate and show weak or absent tryptase staining while retaining strong CD117/KIT staining.

## ustun-2025-systemic-mastocytosis-pdf-C0047

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** What is next after making a diagnosis of SM?; eosinophilia/kinase-fusion paragraph

### Interpretation

In an eosinophilic patient with systemic mastocytosis being evaluated for an associated hematologic neoplasm, a myeloid/lymphoid neoplasm with eosinophilia and tyrosine-kinase fusion must be excluded if no KIT mutation is detected.

## ustun-2025-systemic-mastocytosis-pdf-C0048

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** KIT, PDGFR

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** What is next after making a diagnosis of SM?; eosinophilia/kinase-fusion paragraph

### Interpretation

Rare true systemic mastocytosis with an associated eosinophil neoplasm can harbor both a KIT mutation and a PDGFR rearrangement, so detection of both abnormalities does not by itself exclude coexistence of systemic mastocytosis and the eosinophil neoplasm.

## ustun-2025-systemic-mastocytosis-pdf-C0102

**Category:** biomarker

**Genes:** KIT

**Diseases:** mastocytosis

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Diagnosis and classification; KIT mutation paragraph

### Interpretation

When systemic mastocytosis is morphologically present but standard NGS is negative for KIT, high-sensitivity KIT D816V testing with droplet-digital or allele-specific PCR capable of detecting variants below about 0.1% VAF should be performed to avoid false-negative results.
