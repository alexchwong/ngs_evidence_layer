## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraph 1

**Secondary citation:** Khoury JD, Solary E, Abla O, et al. The 5th edition of the World Health Organization classification of haematolymphoid tumours: myeloid and histiocytic/dendritic neoplasms. Leukemia. 2022;36(7):1703-1719.

### Interpretation

CHIP is defined by a myeloid-associated somatic mutation in blood or bone marrow with VAF ≥2% in an individual without a myeloid neoplasm or unexplained cytopenia.

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0002

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction, paragraph 1

**Secondary citation:** Arber DA, Orazi A, Hasserjian RP, et al. International consensus classification of myeloid neoplasms and acute leukemias: integrating morphologic, clinical, and genomic data. Blood. 2022;140(11):1200-1228.

### Interpretation

In a patient with CHIP, AMC ≥0.5 × 10^9/L, monocytes ≥10% of the WBC differential, and no bone-marrow morphologic findings of CMML further classify the condition as CMUS.
