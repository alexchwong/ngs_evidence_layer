# Xie Z, Komrokji R, Al Ali N, Regelson A, Geyer S, Patel A, et al. Risk prediction for clonal cytopenia: multicenter real-world evidence. Blood. 2024;144(19):2033-2044.

**Publication key:** `xie-2024-ccrs-ccus`

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0001

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction — first paragraph (WHO fifth edition definition)

### Interpretation

According to the WHO fifth edition definition cited by the paper, a myeloid-associated somatic mutation with VAF ≥2% in blood or bone marrow without a myeloid neoplasm is classified as CHIP when unexplained cytopenia is absent and as CCUS when unexplained cytopenia is present.

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** 

**Diseases:** CHIP, CCUS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Introduction — first paragraph (ICC classification)

### Interpretation

According to ICC classification, when absolute monocyte count is ≥0.5 × 10^9/L, monocytes comprise ≥10% of the white blood cell differential, and bone marrow lacks morphologic CMML, CHIP is classified as CMUS and CCUS is classified as CCMUS.

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0005

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2, SRSF2, ZRSR2

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Prognostic factors for outcomes, LFS

### Interpretation

In CCUS, TET2, SRSF2, and ZRSR2 mutations are associated with shorter leukemia-free survival.

**Locator:** Results — Prognostic factors for outcomes, LFS
