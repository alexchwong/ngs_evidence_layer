**Publication key:** `xie-2024-ccrs-ccus`

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1

**Diseases:** myeloid neoplasm, unspecified

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Abstract; Introduction — CHIP/CCUS progression and mutation patterns

### Interpretation

The Introduction cites prior evidence that specific mutation patterns, including comutations involving DNMT3A, TET2, or ASXL1, contribute to diagnosing myeloid neoplasm (MN).

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TET2, SRSF2, ZRSR2

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Methods — Definitions; Results — Prognostic factors for outcomes — LFS (individual-gene analysis)

### Interpretation

In the 357-patient CCUS cohort, TET2, SRSF2, and ZRSR2 mutations were associated with shorter leukemia-free survival (LFS); LFS was defined from CCUS diagnosis to progression to MDS, CMML, or acute myeloid leukemia. Reported HRs were 3.29, 3.81, and 3.19, respectively.

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0003

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results — Prognostic factors for outcomes — LFS (individual-gene analysis)

### Interpretation

In the 357-patient CCUS cohort, DNMT3A mutation was associated with a lower risk of disease transformation than absence of a DNMT3A mutation (HR 0.18, 95% CI 0.04-0.76; P = .02).

## xie-2024-ccrs-ccus-C0004

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** ASXL1, SRSF2, DNMT3A, TET2

**Diseases:** CCUS

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results — Prognostic factors for outcomes — OS (individual-gene analysis)

### Interpretation

In individual-gene analysis of the 357-patient CCUS cohort, ASXL1 and SRSF2 mutations were associated with inferior overall survival (HR 2.5 and 2.31, respectively), whereas DNMT3A and TET2 mutations were not associated with overall survival (HR 0.70, P = .51; and HR 0.99, P = .96, respectively).
