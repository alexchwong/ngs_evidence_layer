## urrutia-2025-tp53-myeloid-C0002

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML, MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** TP53 mutational status and the role of cnLOH; Figure 3 legend; Suggestions for TP53-mutated myeloid neoplasm testing and nomenclature

### Interpretation

In TP53-mutated AML/MDS, findings supporting multihit TP53 status include TP53 cnLOH demonstrated by dedicated testing; loss of chromosome 17 or 17p; two or more TP53 mutations; or a single TP53 mutation with VAF >55% and sequencing coverage >200×. A TP53 VAF <55% does not exclude multihit disease.

## urrutia-2025-tp53-myeloid-C0003

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML, MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** TP53 mutational status and the role of cnLOH

### Interpretation

In TP53-mutated AML/MDS, complex karyotype may suggest multihit TP53 status but is an imperfect surrogate: some TP53-mutated cases with complex karyotype lack chromosome 17 deletion or TP53 cnLOH, whereas some cases with TP53 cnLOH have noncomplex karyotypes.

## urrutia-2025-tp53-myeloid-C0007

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** MDS

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Societal classification of myeloid neoplasms with TP53 mutations, Figure 2

### Interpretation

According to WHO 2022 as summarized by Urrutia et al., MDS with biallelic TP53 inactivation has <20% blasts and no history of cytotoxic or radiation therapy; biallelic TP53 inactivation is defined by two or more TP53 mutations, one TP53 mutation with chromosome 17/17p copy loss, or one TP53 mutation with TP53 cnLOH.

## urrutia-2025-tp53-myeloid-C0016

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** TP53

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** TP53 mutational status and the role of cnLOH

### Interpretation

In TP53-mutated AML undergoing transplantation, complex karyotype is associated with inferior overall survival compared with absence of complex karyotype.
