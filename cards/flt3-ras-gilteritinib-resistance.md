## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0012

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, DNMT3A, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — dual DNMT3A/NPM1 survival benefit (paper.md line 93)

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, concurrent DNMT3A and NPM1 comutations were associated with the most pronounced survival benefit from gilteritinib among the molecular subgroups analyzed.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0013

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, FLT3-ITD features and gilteritinib benefit (paper.md line 231)

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, gilteritinib benefit was maintained across FLT3-ITD allelic-ratio groups, FLT3-ITD lengths, and single versus multiple FLT3-ITD mutations; these FLT3-ITD features did not appear to negate gilteritinib benefit.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0015

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0017

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** BRAF, KRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — targetability of acquired Ras/MAPK mutations (paper.md line 239)

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory AML, the acquired Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations observed after gilteritinib were not amenable to the BRAF V600E- or KRAS G12C-directed mutation-specific inhibitors available at the time of the study.
