# Smith CC, Levis MJ, Perl AE, Hill JE, Rosales M, Bahceci E. Molecular profile of <i>FLT3</i>-mutated relapsed/refractory patients with AML in the phase 3 ADMIRAL study of gilteritinib. Blood Advances. 2022;6(7):2144-2155.

**Publication key:** `flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance`

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0001

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Impact of baseline FLT3-ITD allelic ratio on clinical outcomes

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory AML, no FLT3-ITD allelic-ratio threshold has been established as prognostic; the ADMIRAL cutoff of 0.77 was study-defined rather than a validated prognostic threshold.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0002

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph beginning “Unlike newly diagnosed AML...”

### Interpretation

Within ADMIRAL relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, the study-defined high FLT3-ITD allelic ratio (≥0.77) was associated with shorter overall survival than the low-ratio group across treatment arms; the authors cautioned that 0.77 may be a surrogate for disease burden.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph beginning “Unlike newly diagnosed AML...”

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, gilteritinib showed an overall-survival benefit versus salvage chemotherapy in both high and low FLT3-ITD allelic-ratio groups; allelic ratio did not identify a subgroup lacking benefit.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion, paragraph beginning “Unlike newly diagnosed AML...”

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, FLT3-ITD length did not identify loss of gilteritinib activity: survival favored gilteritinib across length groups and response was not affected by ITD length.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Impact of FLT3-ITD mutation length and multiple FLT3-ITD mutations on clinical outcomes

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, the presence of multiple FLT3-ITD mutations did not adversely affect response to gilteritinib; pretransplant composite complete remission was 52% with gilteritinib versus 32% with salvage chemotherapy.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, DNMT3A, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Impact of baseline comutations on survival outcomes; Discussion

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, concurrent DNMT3A and NPM1 mutations identified the molecular subgroup with the most pronounced survival benefit from gilteritinib in ADMIRAL; the genotype-treatment association should be considered preliminary pending independent confirmation.

**Genes:** FLT3, NRAS, PTPN11, KRAS, CBL

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3, NRAS, PTPN11, KRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, Mutation profiles of patients who relapsed on gilteritinib therapy; Discussion

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML relapsing on gilteritinib, acquired Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations were a common resistance mechanism; NRAS, PTPN11, and KRAS were the most frequently acquired pathway genes.
