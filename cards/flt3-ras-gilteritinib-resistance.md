# Smith CC, Levis MJ, Perl AE, Hill JE, Rosales M, Bahceci E. Molecular profile of <i>FLT3</i>-mutated relapsed/refractory patients with AML in the phase 3 ADMIRAL study of gilteritinib. Blood Advances. 2022;6(7):2144-2155.

**Publication key:** `flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance`

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0001

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Impact of baseline comutations on response outcomes

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML patients randomized within the high-intensity salvage-chemotherapy stratum of ADMIRAL, the DNMT3A-mutated subgroup had a higher pretransplant CR/CRh rate with gilteritinib than with high-intensity salvage chemotherapy (28.0% vs 16.0%).

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0002

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** DNMT3A, NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Impact of baseline comutations on survival outcomes

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML in ADMIRAL, concurrent DNMT3A and NPM1 comutations identified the molecular subgroup with the most pronounced survival benefit from gilteritinib; this apparent benefit persisted among patients eligible for high-intensity chemotherapy, although 43% (16/37) of patients in the gilteritinib arm who were eligible for high-intensity chemotherapy underwent HSCT during the study.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0003

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** WT1, IDH1, IDH2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results — Impact of baseline comutations on survival outcomes

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML patients eligible for high-intensity chemotherapy in ADMIRAL, the WT1- and IDH1/IDH2-comutated subgroups maintained a survival advantage with gilteritinib versus salvage chemotherapy.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0004

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — FLT3-ITD allelic ratio

### Interpretation

In relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML in ADMIRAL, gilteritinib showed survival benefit versus salvage chemotherapy in both high (≥0.77) and low (<0.77) FLT3-ITD allelic-ratio groups; the study used 0.77, its cohort median, because no prognostic cut point had been established for relapsed/refractory AML.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — FLT3-ITD length and number

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated AML treated with gilteritinib in ADMIRAL, FLT3-ITD length ≥51 bp did not appear to worsen survival or response, and multiple FLT3-ITD mutations did not appear to worsen survival; overall gilteritinib benefit was observed regardless of FLT3-ITD allelic ratio, length, or number.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0006

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — FLT3-ITD allelic ratio

### Interpretation

Within ADMIRAL relapsed/refractory FLT3-mutated AML, patients below the study-median FLT3-ITD allelic ratio of 0.77 had longer overall survival than those at or above 0.77 regardless of treatment arm; the authors suggested this threshold may be a surrogate for disease burden and noted that no established prognostic cut point exists for relapsed/refractory AML.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0007

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NRAS, PTPN11, KRAS, CBL

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Abstract; Results — baseline Ras/MAPK pathway mutations

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML, baseline Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations—represented in ADMIRAL by NRAS, PTPN11, KRAS and CBL—did not preclude clinical benefit from gilteritinib; among baseline Ras/MAPK-mutated patients, CR/CRh was 22.2% with gilteritinib versus 14.3% with salvage chemotherapy.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0008

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NRAS, PTPN11, KRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion — mutations at relapse on gilteritinib

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML patients who relapsed on gilteritinib, acquired Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations were associated with treatment resistance; new Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations occurred in 18 of 40 relapsed patients (45%) and most frequently involved NRAS, PTPN11 and KRAS.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0009

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** FLT3

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results/Discussion — mutations at relapse on gilteritinib

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML patients who relapsed on gilteritinib, acquired FLT3 F691L gatekeeper mutations were associated with treatment resistance; they occurred in 5 of 40 evaluable relapses and were mutually exclusive with acquired Ras/MAPK-pathway mutations.

## flt3-ras-gilteritinib-resistance-C0010

**Category:** prognosis

**Genes:** NPM1

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Discussion — NPM1 outcomes

### Interpretation

In FLT3-mutated relapsed/refractory AML in this ADMIRAL analysis, NPM1 mutation had a somewhat negative association with outcomes; the authors caution that genotype-response associations should be considered preliminary until independently confirmed.
