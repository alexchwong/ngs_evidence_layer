## vainchenker-2017-mpn-C0005

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** JAK2 gene mutations, paragraph on JAK2V617F distribution and specificity

### Interpretation

JAK2 V617F supports a classical myeloproliferative neoplasm but is not specific to classical myeloproliferative neoplasms because it can occur in other hematologic malignancies and at very low levels in otherwise healthy individuals or age-related clonal hematopoiesis.

## vainchenker-2017-mpn-C0006

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** JAK2

**Diseases:** PV, ET, PMF

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** JAK2 gene mutations, paragraph on exon 12 mutations

### Interpretation

A JAK2 exon 12 mutation favors polycythemia vera, particularly in JAK2 V617F-negative disease, and is not usually associated with essential thrombocythemia or primary myelofibrosis.

## vainchenker-2017-mpn-C0009

**Category:** diagnosis

**Genes:** MPL, JAK2, CALR

**Diseases:** ET

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Thrombopoietin receptor gene (MPL) mutations, paragraph on noncanonical MPL mutations

### Interpretation

In essential thrombocythemia negative for JAK2 V617F, CALR, MPL W515, and MPL S505N, a noncanonical MPL mutation such as S204, E230, or Y591 is more often acquired and supports a true acquired myeloproliferative neoplasm.

## vainchenker-2017-mpn-C0026

**Category:** germline

**Diseases:** MPN

**Evidence tier:** restated secondary

**Locator:** Predisposition, paragraph on common susceptibility alleles

### Interpretation

Common susceptibility alleles involving JAK2, including the 46/1 haplotype, TERT, MECOM, SH2B3, CHEK2, PINT, and GFI1B confer modest inherited predisposition to myeloproliferative neoplasms, with reported risk increases of approximately twofold to sixfold.
