## idh-comutations-inhibitor-resistance-C0005

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** NRAS

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, 'Co-occurring RUNX1 or RAS signaling mutations are associated with primary resistance'; Discussion

### Interpretation

In AML treated with an IDH inhibitor, baseline NRAS co-mutation trended toward poor response; in the current cohort none of six patients with concurrent NRAS mutation achieved complete remission.

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** multivariable-adjusted

**Locator:** Results, 'Leukemia stemness is associated with primary resistance to IDHi'; Methods, 'LSC17 score calculation'; Discussion, limitations

### Interpretation

In AML treated with IDH inhibitors, a higher 17-gene LSC17 expression score was associated with nonresponse and predicted complete remission (AUROC 0.785); LSC17 remained significant in multivariable analysis, but the finding requires independent validation.

## idh-comutations-inhibitor-resistance-C0013

**Category:** treatment

**Genes:** TET2

**Diseases:** AML

**Evidence tier:** univariable or descriptive

**Locator:** Results, 'Mapping genetic and epigenetic evolution during IDHi therapy'; Discussion

### Interpretation
