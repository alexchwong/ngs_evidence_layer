## Diagnosis and classification

[1f3a650ff233058c-0026] WHO-5 AML, myelodysplasia-related is defined by at
    least 20% blasts plus one or more listed MDS-related cytogenetic or
    molecular abnormalities and/or prior MDS or MDS/MPN; morphology alone is
    removed. The mutation-defined set comprises SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1, ZRSR2,
    ASXL1, EZH2, BCOR and STAG2. [1]

[1f3a650ff233058c-0025] WHO-5 does not recognize AML with somatic RUNX1
    mutation as a standalone type because RUNX1 mutations overlap broadly with
    other defining molecular features and lack sufficient specificity. This is
    a negative classification fact, not a statement that RUNX1 has no other
    diagnostic or prognostic role (negative fact; remove in final pass per
    R1.7). [1]
