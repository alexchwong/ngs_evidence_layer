## Diagnosis and classification

[1f3a650ff233058c-0026] WHO-5 AML, myelodysplasia-related is defined by at
    least 20% blasts plus one or more listed MDS-related cytogenetic or
    molecular abnormalities and/or prior MDS or MDS/MPN; morphology alone is
    removed. The mutation-defined set comprises SRSF2, SF3B1, U2AF1, ZRSR2,
    ASXL1, EZH2, BCOR and STAG2. [1]

[1f3a650ff233058c-0013] WHO-5 recognizes MDS with low blasts and SF3B1
    mutation and MDS with biallelic TP53 inactivation as genetically defined
    MDS types; MDS-biTP53 supersedes MDS-5q and MDS-SF3B1. The quoted passage
    does not state the full blast or exclusion criteria. [1]

[1f3a650ff233058c-0014] For WHO-5 MDS-SF3B1, at least 15% ring sideroblasts
    may substitute for an SF3B1 mutation, with the acceptable related term MDS
    with low blasts and ring sideroblasts. The source does not state the blast
    threshold or exclusion criteria in this quoted footnote. [1]

[1f3a650ff233058c-0018] WHO-5 renames the entity as MDS/MPN with SF3B1
    mutation and thrombocytosis. For SF3B1-wild-type cases, at least 15% ring
    sideroblasts permits the acceptable term MDS/MPN with ring sideroblasts
    and thrombocytosis. The source does not state the full platelet, blast,
    morphology or exclusion criteria. [1]
